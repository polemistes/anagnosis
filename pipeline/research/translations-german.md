# German versions of Anagnosis texts: source research (started 2026-11-14)

Rules: PD in EEA = died 1955 or earlier; US PD = published 1930 or earlier. NC licences unusable.

## 1. Homer, Odyssey

### Translation
1. **Johann Heinrich Voss, 1781 (first edition, "Homers Odüßee")** — Voss d. 1826. PD EEA + US. Hexameter verse, aims at one German line per Greek line (keeps the Greek line count; verified via publisher text of the Latacz bilingual edition and line-numbered online copies) -> line-by-line alignment is realistic.
   - Clean digital text: Deutsches Textarchiv, https://www.deutschestextarchiv.de/voss_oduessee_1781 — double-keyed transcription of the Fraktur first edition, TEI-XML/HTML/plain, licence **CC BY-SA 4.0** (verified on the page; an old http mirror page still shows CC BY-NC 3.0 — old licence, the current one is BY-SA). Page scans included.
   - Spelling is 1781 (Odüßee, übersezt...): archaic, charming, but harder for modern readers.
2. **Voss, revised text (1793 onward; the version everyone reads)** — same PD status. Clean text at Zeno.org (http://www.zeno.org/Literatur/M/Homer/Epen/Odyssee, from a 1976 Winkler edition) and Projekt Gutenberg-DE (from Insel 1990). Caution: both sites sit on modern editions (normalised spelling) and claim rights in their own databases; Projekt Gutenberg-DE terms forbid commercial reuse (unverified detail, typical). Safer: transcribe/OCR a PD revised edition scan (Google Books 1802/1819/1839 listed on de.wikisource Homer page) or use DTA 1781.
3. Others listed on de.wikisource/Homer (scans only, Google Books): Ernst Wiedasch 1830-31, A. L. W. Jacob 1844, J. J. C. Donner 1865 (all PD). No reason to prefer them over Voss.
   No PD German *prose* Odyssey of quality found; Voss is the obvious choice.

### Commentary
- **Ameis–Hentze(–Cauer), "Homers Odyssee für den Schulgebrauch erklärt"**, Teubner, many editions 1856-1920s. Greek text + German footnote commentary, plus separate "Anhang" volumes (critical/longer notes). K. F. Ameis d. 1870; Carl Hentze 1834-1908 (verified, Wikidata); Paul Cauer d. 1921 (verified). All PD EEA; editions to 1930 PD US.
  - Scans: archive.org homersodysseefr00hentgoog (1874-77), bub_gb_SrIgAAAAMAAJ (1908, Cauer), homersodysseefrd0000unse (1879); HathiTrust full view 100533152 (1874-77), 100679557 (1884-90), 100738001 / 002722416 (1908-11). Anhang volumes on archive.org (creator "Ameis, Karl Friedrich, 1811-1870").
  - Scan + OCR only (Greek + German Antiqua mostly; Teubner school editions are in Antiqua, not Fraktur). Dense, line-keyed notes: ideal format for the reader's commentary notes. Very large job (24 books).

### Dictionary
- Autenrieth's original German "Wörterbuch zu den homerischen Gedichten" (see dictionaries section).

## General notes on German digital-text sources (verified 2026-11-14)
- **Deutsches Textarchiv (DTA)**: transcriptions CC BY-SA 4.0 (commercial use OK with attribution + share-alike on the text). Good.
- **TextGrid Digitale Bibliothek** (textgridrep.org): the old Zeno.org *Literatur* corpus, licensed by Editura/Zeno to TextGrid; the markup/metadata are CC BY 3.0 DE (attribution "TextGrid"), the underlying PD texts are free. Usable commercially. Covers Zeno "Literatur" (Homer, Sophokles, Aischylos, Aristophanes ... appear), not, as far as I could see, Zeno "Philosophie" (Plato) or the dictionaries.
- **Zeno.org itself**: terms (fetched 2026-11-14) say PD texts may be reused only for private, non-commercial purposes; commercial use needs written consent of Henricus – Edition Deutsche Klassik GmbH; no crawling; database right claimed. => **Unusable directly** for a paid app (except via TextGrid for the literature corpus, or by asking permission).
- **Projekt Gutenberg-DE** (projekt-gutenberg.org): terms allow private use only; commercial licence for a fee. => **Unusable.**
- **Project Gutenberg (gutenberg.org)**: PD in the US, reuse OK, but check EEA status of each translator (e.g. #24899 Platons Gastmahl is Rudolf Kassner, d. 1959: NOT PD in EEA until 2030).
- **de.wikisource**: transcriptions free (PD/CC BY-SA). Mostly scan lists for Greek authors; few transcriptions.
- **opera-platonis.de** (Rudolf Haller): edited Schleiermacher/Susemihl texts with Stephanus numbers, but the owner claims authorship, no licence => only with permission.
- **BKV (bkv.unifr.ch)**: see Hermas section.

## 2-3. Plato, Symposium and Gorgias

### Translations
1. **Friedrich Schleiermacher** (d. 1834). Gorgias in Platons Werke II.1 (1805), Gastmahl in II.2 (1807); 2nd ed. 1817-28; 3rd ed. 1855-61. PD EEA + US. Prose, close to the Greek; sentence alignment feasible (Stephanus pages must be added by hand or from a later reprint). The classic German Plato.
   - Scans: MDZ (1st ed.: Gorgias bsb10237330, Gastmahl bsb10237331), Google Books (2nd/3rd ed.) – links on https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Platon. No transcription on Wikisource or DTA (DTA has only Teil 1). Clean texts exist only on Projekt Gutenberg-DE (private use only) and opera-platonis.de (permission needed). => OCR needed (1st ed. likely Fraktur? unverified; 3rd ed. 1855-61 probably Antiqua, unverified).
2. **Otto Apelt** (1845-1932, verified), Philosophische Bibliothek (Meiner), "übersetzt und erläutert": Gorgias 1914 (MDZ bsb11171842; HathiTrust 102667255 full view), Sämtliche Dialoge 1919-20, 2nd ed. 1922: Bd. 1 with Gorgias = archive.org smtlichedialog01plat; Bd. 3 with Gastmahl = archive.org smtlichedialog00platuoft. PD EEA (d. 1932) and US (1914/1922). Prose, more modern German than Schleiermacher, with Stephanus numbers in the margin (usual in Meiner; unverified for these scans) and **Apelt's own explanatory notes (Anmerkungen)** = a small German commentary for free. That the Gastmahl in Bd. 3 is Apelt's own translation is unverified (check scan). Antiqua print, OCR straightforward. **Best candidate for both dialogues.**
3. Older alternatives (via Zeno only, so not directly usable): Franz Susemihl Gastmahl 1855 (d. 1901), Julius Deuschle Gorgias 1859 (d. 1861) — Zeno text taken from the 1940 Lambert Schneider "Sämtliche Werke" (Loewenthal). Original prints (Krais & Hoffmann, Stuttgart) would need scans.
   AVOID: Rudolf Kassner's Gastmahl (Gutenberg #24899; Kassner d. 1959).

### Commentaries
- **Symposium: Arnold Hug** (1832-1895), "Platons Symposion erklärt", Teubner (Cron-Deuschle series, Teil 5): 1st ed. 1876, 2nd ed. 1884 (archive.org platonssymposion00plat, platonsausgewhl00bochgoog; HathiTrust 100645910; Google). 3rd ed. 1909 rev. **Hermann Schöne** (d. 1941) on HathiTrust 012289467 / 100628364. All PD EEA; 1876/1884/1909 PD US. Long introduction (LXIV pp.) + full German commentary. Standard work, still cited. Excellent.
  Also Carl Schmelzer, Weidmann 1882 (archive.org platonsausgewhl02platgoog etc.); Harder's 2nd ed. 1915 (Christian Harder, death year unverified).
- **Gorgias: Deuschle–Cron**, "Platons Gorgias für den Schulgebrauch erklärt", Teubner: Julius Deuschle (d. 1861) 1st ed. 1859 (archive.org gorgiasfrdensc00platuoft); Christian Cron (1813-1892) 2nd ed. 1867 (MDZ bsb11089719), 3rd 1876 (archive.org platonsgorgiasf00deusgoog), 4th 1886 (HathiTrust 012468447; Wuppertal). PD. **Avoid the 5th ed. 1909 by Wilhelm Nestle (d. 1959).**
  Also Alfred Gercke (d. 1922), Sauppe series (Weidmann) 1897, archive.org platonsausgewhl00platgoog; Schmelzer (Weidmann) 1883.

## 4. New Testament (SBLGNT)

Alignment is trivial for any Bible translation (verse numbering). The main question is text base: Luther 1912 follows the Textus Receptus tradition (extra verses/words vs SBLGNT); Elberfelder, Weizsäcker and Menge follow critical texts, closer to SBLGNT.

### Translations
1. **Elberfelder 1905** (unrevised; translators of the 1850s-70s, all long dead). PD EEA + US. Very literal, critical text: best fit for a reading aid next to SBLGNT. Clean text widely available (Zefania XML project, BibleStudyTools says PD; eBible.org page not confirmed). Use a source that states PD; check text quality.
2. **Carl Weizsäcker, "Das Neue Testament übersetzt"** (1875; Weizsäcker d. 1899; 9th ed. near his death; used as the NT of Kautzsch's Textbibel 1899). PD EEA + US. Scholarly, critical text, good modern-ish German. Clean text: textbibel.de (chapter pages, typed from Kautzsch's Textbibel; licence not stated — unverified), blumhardt.com offers the 1927 Mohr edition as PDF/plain text/Word (licence not stated). Scans: Google Books 1894 6th/7th ed. (id vobBOtAmfjQC), HathiTrust 009795025 (Textbibel 1899). Probably the best *scholarly* German NT that is PD.
3. **Luther 1912** — PD. Clean text: eBible.org deu1912 (marked Public Domain; USFM/HTML/SWORD). Most familiar to German readers, but TR-based and archaic; warning (toledot.info) that the widespread Zefania 2009 Luther-1912 text has errors.
4. **Menge** (Hermann Menge d. 1939): NT 1909, Bible 1926, last revision 1939. PD in the EEA since 2010 (de.wikipedia). Clean text: GitHub renehamburger/Menge-Bibel (taken from the Deutsche Bibelgesellschaft site with permission, PD Mark) and RootDev4/Deutsche-Menge-Bibel. **US caution**: the commonly distributed 1939 text was published 1939 and is probably still protected in the US (URAA restoration, to end of 2034) — my inference, unverified; the 1909 NT / 1926 Bible would be PD in the US but need OCR.
   AVOID: "Menge 2020" (CLV) and the Schlachter 1951 revision (revisers' rights unclear; original Schlachter 1905 is PD).

### Commentaries
- **Meyer, Kritisch-exegetischer Kommentar (KEK)**, Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht. PD volumes cover the whole NT: Bernhard Weiss (d. 1918) Matthew, Mark/Luke, John (archive.org kritischexegeti01weisgoog, dasjohannesevang00weisuoft 1902), also Romans, Pastorals/Hebrews? (Abt. 11, 13); H. H. Wendt (d. 1928) Acts; C. F. G. Heinrici (d. 1915) 1-2 Cor; Friedrich Sieffert (death unverified, c. 1911) Galatians; Erich Haupt (d. 1910) captivity letters; Wilhelm Bousset (d. 1920) Revelation 1896/1906; Johannes Weiss (d. 1914) 1 Cor 1910; H. A. W. Meyer (d. 1873) original volumes. Later KEK volumes: Dibelius James 1921 (d. 1947, PD), Windisch 2 Cor 1924 (d. 1935, PD), Lohmeyer Phil/Col/Phlm 1930 (d. 1946, PD EEA; 1930 = PD US). Avoid Bultmann John 1941 (d. 1976). Greek-text-based, philological, dense, line-by-line: excellent fit, but huge. Antiqua print; scans on archive.org/HathiTrust/Google. OCR job.
- **"Die Schriften des Neuen Testaments neu übersetzt und für die Gegenwart erklärt"** (ed. Johannes Weiss; 1st ed. 1906-08, 3rd ed. 1917-20 ed. Bousset/Heitmüller), Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht. Gives **a new translation + running popular commentary** in one work. Scans: archive.org 09660486.1409.emory.edu (1907, Bd. 2), HathiTrust 100432585, 100432817, 006279958 (3rd ed., full view). BUT contributor **Wilhelm Lueken (1875-1961, verified)** wrote 1-2 Thess, Phlm, Col, Eph, Phil: those parts are NOT PD in the EEA until 2032. Georg Hollmann (Hebrews, James, Jude/2 Pet) and Franz Koehler (Pastorals) death years unverified. Others PD: J. Weiss d. 1914, Bousset d. 1920, Heitmüller d. 1926, Gunkel d. 1932, Jülicher d. 1938, Baumgarten d. 1934, Knopf d. 1920. Usable for the Gospels, Acts, Romans, Corinthians, Galatians, John, Revelation.
- Others worth noting (unverified details): Zahn, Kommentar zum NT (Theodor Zahn d. 1933); Lietzmann, Handbuch zum NT (HNT; Lietzmann d. 1942; but individual HNT volume authors vary, e.g. Klostermann d. 1963 not PD).

### Dictionary
- **Erwin Preuschen, Vollständiges griechisch-deutsches Handwörterbuch zu den Schriften des NT und der übrigen urchristlichen Literatur** (1910; Preuschen d. 1920). PD EEA + US. Covers Apostolic Fathers too (Hermas!). Scans only (archive.org); no clean digital text found (see dictionaries section). Do NOT use Walter Bauer's revisions (1928ff; Bauer d. 1960).

## 5-11. Sophocles, all seven plays (Jebb's text)

### Translations
1. **J. J. C. Donner, "Sophokles. Deutsch in den Versmaßen der Urschrift"** (1838-39; 8th ed. 1875; Donner 1799-1875). All seven plays. PD EEA + US. Verse in the original metres; the standard 19th-c. complete German Sophocles (used for Mendelssohn's Antigone and Oedipus). Line-for-line closeness: likely close (metre-for-metre translations usually keep lineation), unverified. Scans: Google Books (4th ed. 1856, 6th ed. 1868; listed on https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Johann_Jakob_Christian_Donner), archive.org knigdipus00soph (König Ödipus, Freytag 1895). No clean text found (Wikisource has no Donner Sophocles). OCR needed; type face unverified.
2. **Ulrich von Wilamowitz-Moellendorff, Griechische Tragoedien** (Weidmann 1899ff; d. 1931): only **Oedipus Tyrannus** (Bd. 1) and **Philoctetes** (Bd. 4) from Sophocles. PD EEA (d. 1931) and US (pre-1930 editions). Superb modern verse translations, freer in lineation. Scans: archive.org bub_gb_v6c7IxZDeMAC (1906, all 4 vols?), griechischetrag00moegoog, griechischetrag00aiscgoog, griechischetrag01aescgoog; HathiTrust 100405028.
3. **K. W. F. Solger** (1808; d. 1819): all seven plays; clean text of **König Ödipus and Antigone only** on Zeno (from "Griechische Tragiker", München 1958) => available via TextGrid (CC BY 3.0). Rest scan only. Older diction.
4. Hölderlin's Antigonae and Oedipus der Tyrann (1804, d. 1843) are transcribed on Wikisource, but they are famous poetic re-creations, not reading aids. Not recommended as the parallel text.

### Commentaries (all verified to exist; scan only, Antiqua print)
- **Schneidewin–Nauck, "Sophokles erklärt"**, Weidmann, all seven plays (+ Anhang). F. W. Schneidewin d. 1856; August Nauck d. 1892; new revisions by **Ewald Bruhn** (1862-1936, verified; OT 1910, Elektra 1912, Antigone 1913) and **Ludwig Radermacher** (d. 1952 per memory, unverified here; Aias 10th ed., OC 9th ed. 1909). All PD EEA; editions to 1930 PD US. Scans: archive.org sophokleserklr03soph (OC 1909, Radermacher), HathiTrust 009643351 (Bruhn-era), 009027048 (Nauck-era 1882ff, vols 1-8), 009623370 (9th ed. 1883-99), 100639131 (1860-73). German footnote commentary keyed to lines — the German counterpart of Jebb, ideal format.
- **Gustav Wolff (d. 1878) / Ludwig Bellermann (d. 1915), "Sophokles für den Schulgebrauch erklärt"**, Teubner: Aias, Elektra, Antigone, OT, OC (vol. 5 1883). Philoctetes/Trachiniae not seen. HathiTrust 009792823, 100783498; ULB Düsseldorf (Elektra 1873); Google Play Books.
- Others (unverified scan locations): N. Wecklein's annotated editions (Lindauer, Munich; Wecklein d. 1926); Georg Kaibel, Elektra 1896 (d. 1901), an outstanding scholarly German commentary.

### Verdict detail
Commentary side is strong (Schneidewin-Nauck-Bruhn-Radermacher for all seven). Translation side: Donner for all seven needs OCR; Wilamowitz for OT/Philoctetes; Solger clean for OT/Antigone.

## 12-14. Aeschylus, Oresteia (Agamemnon, Choephoroi, Eumenides)

### Translations
1. **Johann Gustav Droysen, "Des Aischylos Werke"** (Berlin 1832; 3rd ed. 1868; Droysen d. 1884). PD EEA + US. Verse. **Clean text of the whole Orestie on Zeno.org, hence in the TextGrid Digitale Bibliothek** (Aischylos, 6 items incl. "Die Orestie" and Prometheus; print source: München 1958 "Griechische Tragiker" — a modern reprint, probably normalised spelling) => usable under CC BY 3.0 (attribution TextGrid). Line correspondence with the Greek unverified (Droysen is fairly free in the lyrics). Best ready-made option.
2. **Wilamowitz, Griechische Tragoedien Bd. 2: Orestie** (1900; d. 1931). PD EEA + US. Brilliant modern German; scans on archive.org (see Sophocles section: bub_gb_v6c7IxZDeMAC, griechischetrag00aiscgoog etc.). OCR (Antiqua).
3. Donner's Aeschylus (1854ff., d. 1875) — scans only (unverified locations).

### Commentaries
- **N. Wecklein, "Äschylos Orestie, mit erklärenden Anmerkungen"** (Teubner 1888; Wecklein 1843-1926). All three plays, Greek text + German notes. archive.org schylosorestie13aescuoft (parts 1-3), bub_gb_v4QTAAAAYAAJ. PD EEA + US.
- **Wilamowitz, "Aischylos Orestie griechisch und deutsch, 2. Stück: Das Opfer am Grabe"** (Choephoroi; Weidmann 1896): Greek, Wilamowitz's translation, introduction, commentary, appendices. archive.org bub_gb_FlaQAAAAIAAJ; HathiTrust. A great scholarly German commentary for one play.
- **Friedrich Blass (1843-1907)**: "Die Eumeniden des Aischylos. Erklärende Ausgabe" (Weidmann 1907) = archive.org dieeumenidendesa00aescuoft; "Aischylos' Choephoren, erklärende Ausgabe" (Niemeyer 1906) = HathiTrust (US-only access). PD.
- Agamemnon: Schneidewin–Hense (Weidmann; Otto Hense d. 1931) exists per memory, unverified scan location. Wecklein covers Agamemnon anyway.
- Note: Sidgwick (the current English commentary) has a German equivalent for every play: Wecklein for all three; Wilamowitz/Blass for Choephoroi; Blass for Eumenides.

## 15. Aristophanes, Clouds (Hall-Geldart)

### Translations
1. **J. J. C. Donner, "Die Lustspiele des Aristophanes"**, Bd. 1, 1861 (Donner d. 1875). PD EEA + US. Verse. **Fully transcribed on de.wikisource**: https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Die_Wolken_(Aristophanes) — status "fertig" (proofread twice against the scan), line numbers every 5 lines, scan page numbers. One gap in the source ("(Lücke.)" at a choral song after Act 3 sc. 2 — the print itself). Wikisource text is free to reuse. Best option: clean, numbered, PD.
2. **Ludwig Seeger** (1845-48; d. 1864). Clean text on Zeno/**TextGrid** (https://textgridrep.org/browse/k1fb.0), from the 1952 Artemis "Sämtliche Komödien" (Seeger's translation; Otto Weinreich d. 1972 wrote the introduction and edited — the text itself is Seeger's; do not take Weinreich's introduction). Lively rhymed verse, freer; alignment by speech, not line. CC BY 3.0 via TextGrid.
3. Others (scans): J. G. Droysen 1835-38; Wieland (Wolken, 1798/1813 vol.); Schnitzer; Minckwitz (MDZ).

### Commentaries
- **Theodor Kock (1820-1901), "Ausgewählte Komödien des Aristophanes erklärt", Bd. 1: Die Wolken**, Weidmann: 1852, 2nd ed. 1862, 4th ed. 1894 (reviewed CR 1895). Scans: archive.org ausgewhltekomdi00unkngoog (4 plays in one), ausgewhltekom01aris (1862, vols 1-2); Google Books fS9ZAAAAcAAJ. PD EEA + US. Standard German school/scholarly commentary.
- Otto Kaehler, "Die Wolken des Aristophanes", 2nd ed. Weimar 1887 (archive.org + Google per de.wikisource; whether it has commentary unverified; Kaehler's death year unverified).

## 16. Hesiod, Theogony

### Translations
1. **Rudolf Peppmüller, "Hesiodos. Ins Deutsche übertragen und mit Einleitungen und Anmerkungen versehen"** (Halle, Waisenhaus 1896; Peppmüller d. June 1911 per de.wikisource). PD EEA + US. Hexameter verse (line-for-line closeness likely but unverified). RE (Pauly-Wissowa) called it "die beste deutsche Übertragung seit Voß". Includes introductions and **notes (Anmerkungen)**. Clean text of the Theogony on Zeno.org (pp. 100-146) — but Zeno's terms forbid commercial reuse, and it is probably not in the TextGrid snapshot (TextGrid shows only a Hesiod biography). Scan: HathiTrust 012468388 (full view, Cornell). OCR of ~47 pages is small.
2. **J. H. Voss, "Hesiods Werke und Orfeus der Argonaut"** (Heidelberg 1806; d. 1826). PD. Scans: Google Books = archive.org (links on https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Hesiod); clean text only on Projekt Gutenberg-DE (unusable).
3. Eduard Eyth, "Hesiod's Werke verdeutscht im Versmaße der Urschrift" (1858, 2nd ed. 1865; Google Books). PD (Eyth d. 1884, per memory).
   Avoid Thassilo von Scheffer 1938 (d. 1951: PD in EEA, but 1938 publication probably still protected in the US).

### Commentaries
- **G. F. Schoemann (d. 1879), "Die hesiodische Theogonie ausgelegt und beurtheilt"** (Weidmann 1868, 308 pp.; reviewed by Nietzsche). German interpretive commentary (format — running exposition rather than lemma notes — unverified). Scans: Google Books x2, one mirrored on archive.org (links on de.wikisource Hesiod page).
- Peppmüller's own notes (above).
- No German line-by-line school commentary on the Theogony found (school editions concentrated on Works and Days).

## 17. Lucian, A True Story (Verae Historiae)

### Translations
1. **August Friedrich Pauly, "Lucian's Werke", Bd. 6 (Stuttgart, Metzler 1827), pp. 684-748** (Pauly 1796-1845, per memory). PD EEA + US. Prose. **Fully transcribed on de.wikisource**: https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Wahre_Geschichten — status "fertig" (proofread twice), both books, **sections numbered** (standard paragraph numbers), Pauly's footnotes included (some quote Wieland). Free to reuse. Sentence alignment with Nilén is easy.
2. **C. M. Wieland, "Lucian von Samosata Sämtliche Werke"** (1788-89; d. 1813). PD. The classic German Lucian, elegant but free. Scans: Google Books (vol. 1 bwI6AAAAMAAJ, vol. 2 lgI6AAAAMAAJ, vol. 3 KtyBQttDl-UC); Fraktur, OCR needed. Clean text only via modern editions (Zeno: dialogues only, from Berlin/Weimar 1981; not Wahre Geschichte).
3. Theodor Fischer (1866-67, Google Books), M. Weber 1910-13 (archive.org bub_gb_gSBCAAAAIAAJ, bub_gb_qSBCAAAAIAAJ; Weber's death year unverified). Avoid Hanns Floerke's 1911 revision (Floerke d. 1944? unverified).

### Commentaries
- **Friedrich Gotthold Schoene, "Lucian's Traum, Anacharsis, Demonax, Timon, doppelte Anklage und Wahre Geschichte für den Schulgebrauch mit erklärenden Anmerkungen"** (Halle 1838). PD (death year unverified, but certainly before 1955 given an 1838 publication by an adult schoolmaster). Google Books CmgZAAAAYAAJ. Old and thin, but a real German school commentary.
- Sommerbrodt (Weidmann, 3 vols) and Jacobitz (Teubner) school editions do NOT contain the Wahre Geschichte (Sommerbrodt's contents verified; Jacobitz vol. 2 contents unverified).

## 18. Shepherd of Hermas (Lake's Loeb)

### Translations
1. **Martin Dibelius, "Der Hirt des Hermas" erklärt** (Handbuch zum NT, Ergänzungsband: Die Apostolischen Väter IV; Mohr, Tübingen 1923, pp. 415-644; Dibelius 1883-1947). **Translation + full commentary in one volume** (ThLZ 1923 calls it a full commentary). PD EEA (since 2018) and US (1923). Scan: HathiTrust (US access only, per Online Books Page); no archive.org copy found => scan source must be found (Google Books? a library digitisation, or scan a purchased copy). OCR then needed. The best German Hermas there is.
2. **Franz Zeller, "Die Apostolischen Väter"** (BKV 1. Reihe Bd. 35, Kösel 1918, pp. 179-289). Clean text on **BKV**: https://bkv.unifr.ch/de/works/cpg-1052/versions/der-hirte-des-hermas-bkv — downloadable as DOCX/EPUB/PDF/MD; BKV's copyrights page says the texts are, to the owner's knowledge, out of copyright and may be used freely (attribution requested). Divided per chapter and already aligned to the Loeb Greek on BKV (compare view). **Zeller's death year unverified** — must be checked (if he died after 1955, the translation is not yet PD in the EEA even though 1918 makes it PD in the US). Google Books Neyaa8R9bo4C (1918, full view).
3. Heinrich Weinel's translation in Hennecke, Neutestamentliche Apokryphen (1904; 2nd ed. 1924; Weinel d. 1936) — PD; scan location unverified.

### Commentary
- Dibelius 1923 (above) — excellent, modern, philological.
- BKV includes Zeller's introduction ("Einleitung zum Hirte des Hermas").

### Dictionary
- Preuschen 1910 covers the Apostolic Fathers (see dictionaries).

## OVERALL VERDICT

Cross-cutting points:
- German is far better served than most languages: every text has a PD German translation, and most have a PD German commentary from the Weidmann/Teubner school-edition tradition (Antiqua print, Greek lemmata, line-keyed notes: the same shape as Jebb/Sidgwick).
- Clean, reusable digital texts are the bottleneck. Usable: de.wikisource (free), DTA (CC BY-SA 4.0: share-alike applies to the German text), TextGrid (CC BY 3.0, the old Zeno literature corpus), BKV (free use stated), eBible/Zefania/GitHub Bible texts (PD). NOT usable: Zeno.org directly (non-commercial only), Projekt Gutenberg-DE (private use only), opera-platonis.de (no licence), Gutenberg #24899 Kassner (d. 1959).
- No German dictionary exists as clean licensed text. A German word sheet needs OCR of Gemoll 1908 (general), Preuschen 1910 (NT, Hermas) and/or German Autenrieth (Homer). Pape would be ideal but only Zeno has it digitally (commercial use needs Zeno's permission — worth asking). Until then a German edition could keep the English dictionaries.
- All commentaries are scan-only: OCR + Greek lemma checking, as for the English ones.
