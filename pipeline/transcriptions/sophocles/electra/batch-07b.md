753: the print has ἔστε as one word, with smooth breathing and acute on ε (looked at twice, enlarged); ours ἔς τέ.
Note 752: the lemma φορούμ-ενος is divided at the line end in the print; joined.
Note 754: L's κατασχέθοντεσ and, in 757, εὐθὺσ are printed with the medial σ at the end, as L writes; kept.
Note 757: the form ascribed to A, κῄαντες, and κῄοντες have a small iota subscript under the η (enlarged; fainter in κῄοντες); the first κήαντες (L, etc.) has none. κείαντες has the acute on ι.
The lemmas are in the ordinary (italic) Greek type, not bold.
