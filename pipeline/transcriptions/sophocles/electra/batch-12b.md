--- notes
The lemmas are in the ordinary (italic) Greek type, not bold.
1433: the print marks the gap after γεγηθώς with the metrical signs –́ ⏑ – ⏑ –́ ⏑ – (seven signs; the first and fifth long signs carry an ictus accent, written as an en dash with a combining acute), where ours has dots; γεγηθώς has the acute (looked at twice, enlarged). The coronis of τἀν (1424a) checked enlarged: it agrees with ours.
Numbering: the margin numbers 1425 and 1430 agree with ours, but Jebb counts the printed line παύσασθε· … ἐκ προδήλου as two verses, 1428–1429 (his note 1428 f. λεύσσω; the commentary has 1429 ἐκ προδήλου), and our text has no line 1427: so his 1427 (μητρῷον, note 1427) is our 1428 and his 1428 f. (note on λεύσσω) our 1429.
Note 1423 (continued): L's θυηλῆσ is printed with the medial σ at the end, as L writes; kept.
