--- apparatus
+ θάλλοντά γ’ r.

## 956
κατοκνήσεις r: κατοκνήσηισ L. The subjunctive was the prevalent reading, and is that of Ald.

## 957
This v. (cited by the schol. on Eur. *Hec.* 570) is rejected by Wunder and Nauck; Bergk would place it before 956.
--- notes
The lemmas are in the ordinary (italic) Greek type, not bold.
958: the print has ἐς τίν’ (smooth breathing and grave on ἐς, looked at twice, enlarged); ours εἰς τίνʼ.
965: the acute on γένος is barely visible in the print (only a faint trace, worn type); not reported as a difference.
Note 956: L's κατοκνήσηισ is printed with the iota adscript and the medial σ at the end, as L writes; kept.
