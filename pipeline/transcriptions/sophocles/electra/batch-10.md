## 1097
ἄριστα τᾷ Διὸς εὐσεβείᾳ MSS. (except T), and Ald.: for Διὸς Triclinius wrote Ζηνὸς. In L, the schol. on 1095 notices a *v. l.* ἐριστὰ for ἄριστα: the schol. on 1097 has γρ. ἄριστα ταζηνόσ. For Διὸς, Gleditsch and Michaelis conj. πατρὸς. Wecklein writes ἄριστον αἶνον δι’ εὐσέβειαν: L. Lange conj. κράτος τ’ ἀριστεῖά τ’ εὐσεβείᾳ: Nauck (formerly), ἄριστα τὰν σὰν δι’ εὐσέβειαν: Bellermann, ἐπιστάτα Ζηνὸς εὐσέβειαν.
--- notes
The page begins with the last verse of the antistrophe (6 ἄριστα τᾷ Ζηνὸς εὐσεβείᾳ); the small 6 is Jebb's verse number within the antistrophe, not listed.
Numbering: Jebb's note numbers ἄριστα τᾷ Ζηνὸς εὐσεβείᾳ 1097 (our 1096), so his 1098 ἆρ’ and 1099 ὀρθῶς are our 1097 and 1098 (we have no 1099); the margin numbers 1100 and 1105 agree with ours.
1096: the line ends with a comma after εὐσεβείᾳ, although the ode ends here (looked at twice, enlarged); ours has a full stop.
1097 and 1107: the comma after εἰσηκούσαμεν and the full stop after τινες looked at twice (enlarged).
Note 1097: the lemma is in the ordinary (italic) Greek type, not bold; L's ταζηνόσ is printed with the medial σ at the end, as L writes; kept.
