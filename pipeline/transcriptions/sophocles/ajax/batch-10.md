## 966
ἐμοὶ πικρὸσ τέθνηκεν ἢ κείνοισ γλυκύσ L. The accent on ἢ is from a later hand: the scribe wrote either ἠ or ἦ. The ὸ of πικρὸσ is partially erased, and the σ almost wholly. Four dots ·:· are placed before the verse. There is no variant in the other MSS. The Aldine has a comma after τέθνηκεν (as well as after γλυκύς).—For ἢ Schneidewin wrote ᾗ: Reiske, Porson and Elmsley conj. εἰ: Hartung writes ὡς: Blaydes, καὶ.

## 994
ὁδόσθ’ made in L from ὁδόστ’.—In L the 1st hand wrote ἀπασῶν, but the initial α has been erased (the breathing ’ remains). The later MSS. are divided between πασῶν (A, D, E, T, etc.), and ἀπασῶν (Γ, Pal., etc.). In those which have the latter, the order of words is left unchanged, as in Ald.: ὁδός θ’ ὁδῶν ἀπασῶν ἀνιάσασα δή. A few of the inferior MSS. have ὁδός τ’ ἀνιάσασα δὴ πασῶν ὁδῶν: and so Turnebus.
