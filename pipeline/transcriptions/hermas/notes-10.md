## Mandate 6.1.1
**their workings are twofold**: Here and in *Mand.* viii. Hermas, like Kebes (p. 45), teaches that some things reputed absolutely good or the contrary are in the abstract indifferent, and may be actually either good or evil. There are two ways in everything. Shakespeare makes Hamlet speak of the *steep and thorny way that leads to heaven*. In Hesiod the way to virtue is at first rough: in the *Shepherd* it is “neither rough nor thorny.”
