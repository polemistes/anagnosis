The edition owes much also to Professor Seymour, who has read all the proof, and has cheerfully given the editor the benefit of his scholarship and editorial experience.

Reference is made throughout to Riddell’s *Digest of Platonic Idioms* (Rid.), printed with his edition of the *Apology* (Oxford, 1877). Occasional references are made to Madvig’s *Griechische Syntax*, and to Meisterhans’ *Grammatik der Attischen Inscriften.*

Bryn Mawr College, October, 1890.
