*Gorgias of Leontini* But during the same period, from 427 on, another, more popular, teacher of Rhetoric was coming from time to time to Athens, Gorgias of Leontini, an exponent of the Sicilian rhetoric, with its elaborate arguments from probability, but still more prominently the exponent of a new method of expression. Poetry had the beauty of the grouping of words in symmetrical verses determined by meter ; Gorgias developed a form of prose in which short clauses of almost or quite equal length were ranged in pairs, each pair marked by an antithesis of thought, and often by rhyme of the final syllables. Poetry had also the beauty of a vocabulary of its own, raised above the common speech, and enriched by the free word formations of the poet ; Gorgias transferred this rich vocabulary to his prose.
