**εἶτα** κτλ.: as often, the emphasis is on the participle (*Ach.* 202 n.), ‘provided you obey your father, you may go to the devil’; according to Kock etc., an hyperbaton for πιθόμ. εἶτα ἐξ., but this view is certainly wrong here, and the hyperbaton is not demonstrated by the instances quoted in support of it; e.g. 1249 sq., *Plut.* 79 εἶτ᾽ ἐσίγας Πλοῦτος ὤν; Amphis ii. p. 247 K.
