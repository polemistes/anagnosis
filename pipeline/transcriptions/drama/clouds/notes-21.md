## 762
**ἀποχάλα**: viz. ἕλκε ἄνω τὴν ψυχήν (cp. 228 sq. n.), without, however, losing your hold upon actuality. The metaphor is developed in Plato, Phaedo, 81 A, where Socrates describes true philosophy as a μελέτη θανάτου, which the soul may practise by fleeing from the body and having no communication with it, but συνηθροισμένη αὐτὴ εἰς ἑαυτήν, and meditating on heaven; cp. also Theaet. 173 E.
