## 1155

**ὀβολοστάται**: a term of contempt, like τοκογλύφοι, *toculliones* (Cic. *Att.* ii. 1 § 12), ἡμεροδανεισταί. Though money-lenders, as a class, were not unpopular at Athens (Dem. xxxvii. §§ 53 sqq.), petty usurers, who speculated in distress, were detested (Aristot. *Pol.* i. 10=1258 b 2 εὐλογώτατα μισεῖται ἡ ὀβολοστατική); so much so, that the fact that a man was a money-lender of this kind was sufficient to prejudice him in the eyes of a jury (Dem. ib. § 52).
