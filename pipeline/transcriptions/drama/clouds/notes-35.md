**κιθαρίζειν**: the κιθάρα for a time fell into disuse as compared with the αὐλός (a more passionate instrument), but not for long, as the leaders of fashion, like Alcibiades, soon revolted from it, as being ἀγεννὲς καὶ ἀνελεύθερον, since it spoiled the expression and checked conversation (Freeman, *Schools* etc. pp. 110 sqq.).
