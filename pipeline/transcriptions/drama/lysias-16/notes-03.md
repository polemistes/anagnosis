## 20
**ἠσθόμην**: empirical aorist. “When the aorist has a temporal adverb or a negative or a numeral with it, it is best referred to the same class with the English perfect of experience (empirical aorist),” GS. 259. **νεώτερος**: the young Athenian attained his majority in his nineteenth year, but for two years his service as cadet in garrison (see Gulick 8g f.) almost necessarily precluded his exercising the privileges of a citizen. From his twentieth year on he might take any part in the Ecclesia which his modesty permitted.
