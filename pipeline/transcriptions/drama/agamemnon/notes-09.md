## 1495

## 1498
**μηδ᾽ ἐπιλεχθῇς**, ‘but do not think.’ ἐπιλέγομαι is an Ionic word, used often in Herodotus for ‘to think,’ ‘expect,’ ‘consider,’ properly no doubt ‘to say over to one’s self.’ This tense does not occur elsewhere, and has been suspected; but there is no more intrinsic difficulty in it than in the deponent διελέχθην, which is common.

**μηδέ**, ‘but not,’ is Epic; in Attic it was always ἀλλὰ μή. See Od. 5. 177, 10. 342.

## 1501
