--- apparatus
1433	ταῦτʼ] ταὔτʼ Heath, and so Buttmann.
1436	συννόμω] συννόμωι L.
1437-1440	ἐγὼ δʼ...ἁλῶναι. Jacob (Quaest. Sophocl., 1822) suspected that these words were spurious; this is also the view of Leutsch (Philol. XI. 777). Schenkel (Zeitschr. f. die Oesterr. Gymn., 1876, p. 699) would recast the whole passage from 1431 to 1441.
1440	ἐννοεῖθʼ Elmsley: ἐννοεῖσθʼ MSS.
1441	πορθῆτε] πορθεῖτε L, with ῆ written over εῖ by S.
1442-1444	ὡς τἄλλα...ἀπόλλυται. Dindorf rejects these three vv.
