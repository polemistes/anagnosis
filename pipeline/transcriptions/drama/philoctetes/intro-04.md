## § 19 — Fénelon’s *Télémaque*.

Once, at least, in modern literature the story of Philoctetes has been treated with a really classical grace. The mind of Fénelon was in natural sympathy with the spirit of ancient Greek poetry ; and the twelfth book of the *Télémaque*, where Philoctetes relates his fortunes to Telemachus, is marked by this distinction.
