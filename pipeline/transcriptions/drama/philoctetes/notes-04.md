Wings were the distinctive attribute of Νίκη in art: and Athenians were familiar with the winged Νίκη which the chryselephantine Athena of Pheidias, in the Parthenon, held in her outstretched right hand (cp. Ar. *Av.* 574).

## 170
**μή του**. The force of μή (as dist. from οὐ) is here ‘generic,’ *i.e.*, it presents the situation as typical of a class; ‘in *a* case *where* there is none to tend’: and this implies the *cause* of pity,—‘seeing that there is none to tend.’ Cp. 256, 715: *O. T.* 397 n.

## 174
