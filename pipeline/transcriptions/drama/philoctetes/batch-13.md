--- apparatus
942	προσθείσ L. Dübner thinks that this has been made from προθείσ, and Campbell indicates the same view, though doubtfully (‘προθείς L?’). But προσθείσ is wholly in the writing of the 1st hand. The supposition that he inserted σ after writing προ θείσ seems excluded by the length of the space between ο and θ,—even allowing for his occasional eccentricities in this respect (cp. O. C., Introd. p. xlvi). If, then, he first intended to write προθείσ, the present first σ of προσθείσ must have been his inchoate θ: but there is no trace of erasure. It appears improbable, therefore, that he ever meant anything else than προσθείσ.—προθείς r, Ald., Turnebus, Brunck, Herm., Wunder.
