9	θάκοισιν MSS., which Elmsley keeps, with the older edd.: θάκησιν Seidler, and so most of the recent edd. This conject. is also in R (cod. 34, Riccardian Library, a MS. of the 16th cent., acc. to P. 11	πυθοίμεθα MSS., Campbell: πυθώμεθα Brunck, Elms., and most edd.
13	ἃν δʼ] δʼ, which is not in the MSS., was supplied by Elmsley. The MSS. have either ἃν (as L and A), or χἂν (as B). The double crasis χἂν for καὶ ἃ ἂν is not a difficulty (cp. Ar. Th. 90 χἂν δέῃ, Eur. Her. 173 χοὒν μέσῳ χρόνος, Theocr. 1. 109 χὤδωνις, Hippon. fr. 30 κὠπόλλων): and χἂν is preferred by Blaydes. But, as Elmsley says, ‘veri similius est excidisse δʼ, quod toties apud tragicos excidit.’ In O. T. 749 ἃν δʼ is a variant for ἃ δʼ ἂν, and there, as here, it has been preferred by most of the recent edd. In L the 1st hand had written ἀν, which a corrector changed to ἃν.
15	στέγουσιν MSS.: στέφουσιν Wakefield (‘non male fortasse,’ Linwood), followed by Wunder, Hartung, Blaydes.
