## 239
**ῥιπτεῖ** has the support of the MSS., not only here, but also in 47¢. 131, Z7. 780. In the latter place, it is confirmed by Athenaeus p. 65 F, for he has ῥιπτοῦντα in his paraphrase. ῥιπτεῖν, used only in pres. and impf., was current in Attic (Ar. Eccl. 507 ῥιπτεῖτε χλαίνας).
