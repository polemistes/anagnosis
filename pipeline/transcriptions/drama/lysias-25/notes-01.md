**δή**: Lysias seldom uses δή. In the eight speeches of this volume there are seven instances of καὶ μὲν δή (see on 12. 30) and only eight of 8; in other connections. Lysias’s sparing use of this vivid and emphatic particle is quite in keeping with the simplicity and moderation of his style (cp. on πάνυ 19. 15). The instances of δὴ are the following: (A) To emphasize a preceding word, 12. 34, 12. 62, 22. §, all with imperatives, a common usage; 34. 1 τότε δή. (B) To emphasize the whole statement, 12. 35, 12.5 35,5 12.0057. (C). To markia fact as a familiar one, 25. 9; this usage is in other writers especially common with relatives.
