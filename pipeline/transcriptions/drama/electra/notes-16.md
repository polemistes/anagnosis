**παρουσίαν** here implies the notion of ‘support,’ ‘aid,’ as παρεῖναι often does (2%. 373 n.).—kal σύ που: Cp. 55.

## 950
**λελείμμεθον** is the only classical instance of a 1st pers. dual except περιδώμεθον in 71. 23. 485, and ὁρμώμεθον in Ph. 1079, where 1 have discussed these forms, and given my reasons for thinking that at least the two Sophoclean examples are genuine.—Cp. Ant. 58 μόνα δὴ νὼ λελειμμένα.

## 952
**θάλλοντ᾽ ἔτ᾽** is Reiske’s certain correction of θάλλοντά τ΄. Hermann, retaining the latter, followed Triclinius in taking βίῳ as=év βίῳ ὄντα, ‘alive,’ which is clearly untenable; as is also the conjecture βιοῦν.

**ΟΡ**. 77. 235 kal ζῶντα καὶ θάλλοντα (n.).

## 953
**πράκτορ᾽**, one who exacts ἃ penalty ; an avenger: as the Erinyes are πράκτορες αἵματος (Aesch. Zum. 310). At Athens the πράκτορες were officials who collected fines and penalties (ἐπιβολαί, τιμήματα) imposed by law. We have a similar figurative use of a technical term in Az. 508 μητέρα πολλῶν ἐτῶν κληροῦχον : and in Aesch. Ewum. 273 the word for an ‘auditor’ who examined the accounts of magistrates is applied to the king of the nether world ; μέγας yap “Αιδης ἐστὶν εὔθυνος βροτῶν. There are Shakespearian parallels; e.g., Lear (act 3, sc. 2, 59) calls the raging elements ‘ these dreadful summoners ’ (officers who warned offenders to appear in court): Hamlet (act 5, sc. 2, 347) speaks of ‘this fell sergeant, death’: and death is ‘that fell arrest without all bail’ (Sonz. 74, 1).

## 954
**εἰς σὲ δὴ βλέπω** : here δὴ, after ἡνίκ᾽ οὐκέτ᾽ ἔστιν, marks the next resource, just as in Ant. 173 ἐγὼ κράτη δὴ... ἔχω follows ὅτ᾽ οὖν ἐκεῖνοι.. ὦλοντο. Thus it is here rather an equivalent for ἤδη than merely a mode of emphasising σέ.
