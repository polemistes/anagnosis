The mistake probably arose from some copyist who was thinking of the great chorus in Agamemnon, where Kassandra calls Klytaemnestra ‘a snare,” ἀλλ᾽ ἄρκυς ἡ ξύνευνος, etc. Ag. 1116 (so that he thought ψιν did really refer to Klytaemnestra). and again of the passage in Kassandra’s speech 1232 τί νιν καλοῦσα δυσφιλὲς δάκος τύχοιμ᾽ av; etc.
