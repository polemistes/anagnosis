@ Χορός
1347	πολλῷ τὸ φρονεῖν εὐδαιμονίας
1348	πρῶτον ὑπάρχει· χρὴ δὲ τά γʼ εἰς θεοὺς

--- apparatus
1339-1346	L divides thus: ἄγοιτʼ— | ὃς, ὦ παῖ— | ὃς, σέ τʼ— | ὅπᾱ— | πάντα— | λέχρια— | πότμος...εἰσήλατο. |
1339	ἐκποδών] ἐκ ποδῶν L, with μ above κ from first hand.
1340	σέ τʼ r: σέ γʼ L.—κατέκτανον MSS.: κατέκανον Wilhelm Schneider: κάκτανον Hermann: ἔκτανον Musgrave.
1341	σέ τʼ αὐτὰν L: σέ τʼ αὖ τάνδʼ Seidler. L has ὃσ before σέ τʼ, doubtless by inadvertent repetition from 1340: Hermann deleted it.
1342	ὅπᾱ πρὸσ πρότερον ἴδω· πᾶ καὶ θῶ· | L. For πρότερον, some of the later MSS. (including A) have πότερον. For καὶ θῶ, Musgrave conjectured κλιθῶ. ὅπᾳ was first omitted by Seidler. See Appendix.
1344	λέχρια τάδʼ ἐν χεροῖν L, and so most of the later MSS.: for τάδʼ, Aug. b and Dresd. a give τά τʼ. Brunck gave λέχρια τἀν χεροῖν: Kayser, λέχρια τὰ πρὸ χεροῖν.
1347-1353	These six verses are rejected by Fr. Ritter.
1349	τά τʼ εἰσ θεοὺσ L. For τά τʼ Triclinius gave τά γʼ.—Dindorf writes χρὴ δʼ ἐς τὰ θεῶν: Blaydes, χρὴ δʼ ἐς τοὺς θεοὺς: also conjecturing (as Wecklein does, Ars Soph. em. p. 167) χρὴ δὲ τὰ πρὸς θεούς.
--- notes
1340	Jebb prints *κατέκανον (asterisk left out); the check text had κάκτανον (Hermann's conjecture, named in Jebb's apparatus).
1341	Jebb prints a raised stop after μέλεος (check text: comma); ὤμοι with acute as printed. The asterisk before αὖ left out.
1342	Jebb prints πρὸς πότερον ἴδω, πᾷ *κλιθῶ· πάντα γὰρ: he omits ὅπᾳ (see his apparatus and commentary) and reads πότερον ἴδω, πᾷ where the check text had ὅπᾳ ... πότερα; line rewritten from the page. The printed line covers 1342-1344 (his margin 1345 stands at λέχρια), so it keeps the number 1342.
1344	Jebb's note "1344 f." names λέχρια τάδʼ ἐν χεροῖν, which stands in our 1345 (his margin 1345); written under 1344 as printed.
1345	τἀν with coronis checked at enlargement (as printed).
1348	Raised stop after ὑπάρχει as printed (check text: full stop). This printed line covers 1348-1349; Jebb's note 1349 (τά τʼ εἰσ θεοὺσ L) names its second half, so the note stays 1349.
1350	Raised stop after ἀσεπτεῖν as printed (check text: full stop). The verse ends with λόγοι on this page (margin 1350); μεγάλας πληγὰς τῶν ὑπεραύχων, which the draft had joined to it, is the next printed verse, at the top of p-0306, numbered 1351 by Jebb's apparatus there.
1342	ὅπᾱ in the apparatus (L's reading, twice) is printed with a macron over the α; written ᾱ. In "ὅπᾳ was first omitted" the iota subscript is printed.
1347	"1347—1353 These six verses": Jebb's numbers; with his margin 1350 and his notes 1349 and 1351 they give 1347, 1348(-1349), 1350, 1351, 1352, 1353 for the six printed verses. (His commentary on p-0306 heads a note "1352 γήρᾳ" and on p-0305 "1349 ff. μεγάλοι...λόγοι", which do not agree with this; the margin and the apparatus were followed.)
