It does not prove this,—nor the reverse. τῆς μελλογάμου in Pollux should be (as Semitelos saw) τὴν μελλόγαμον, and we should refer his words solely to v. 633. His point is simply that ἡ μελλόνυμφος is more correct than ἡ μελλονύμφη.—Curtius connects τᾶλις with τέρ-ην, tender; θρόνα, flowers: Sanskrit *tár-una-s*, youthful, tender, *tál-unī*, girl, young woman.
