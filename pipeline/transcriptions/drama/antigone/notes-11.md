## 486
**ἀδελφῆς**, (child of) a sister, ἀδελφιδῆ : for the gen., cp. 380, 825.

**εἴθ᾽ ὁμαιμονεστέρα**, ‘or nearer in blood to me than any member of my family.” The gen. ὁμαιμονεστέρας (see cr. n.) would mean, ‘or (child of) one nearer in blood to me,” etc. She could be the child of no one nearer than a sister, unless it were of a mother or of a daughter; and it is far-fetched to suppose that Creon means, ‘my niece,—aye, my sister, or my granddaughter.’ All that he means is, ‘my niece,—aye, or the nearest relation possible.” This is more simply and clearly said by the nom. ὁμαιμονεστέρα.
