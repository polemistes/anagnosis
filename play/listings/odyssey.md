## Full description (1248)

Homer's Odyssey in the original Greek, to read in Anagnosis, the free ancient Greek reading app. This app adds the text to Anagnosis: install the free Anagnosis app from Google Play as well, and the text appears on its front page.

WHAT YOU GET
• The Greek text of T. W. Allen's Oxford edition (2nd edition, 1917–1919) with his critical apparatus and preface
• A. T. Murray's English translation (1919), a tap away on every page
• The commentary of W. W. Merry and J. Riddell (books 1–12) and D. B. Monro (books 13–24)
• The syntax of the sentences, annotated by hand (Ancient Greek Dependency Treebank)
• Every word linked to its dictionary form, its inflection, Autenrieth's Homeric Dictionary and the Liddell–Scott dictionaries

HOW IT WORKS
Tap a word to see its meaning and form; long press it to see the syntax of its sentence. Switch between the Greek and the English translation, and open the critical apparatus or the commentary for the page you are reading. Choose the book to read from the menu; turning the page past the end of a book goes on to the next.

Everything works offline. The text and its notes come from open data and public-domain editions, and are published openly, like the app's code, at github.com/polemistes/anagnosis.
