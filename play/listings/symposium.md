## Full description (1208)

Plato's Symposium in the original Greek, to read in Anagnosis, the free ancient Greek reading app. This app adds the text to Anagnosis: install the free Anagnosis app from Google Play as well, and the text appears on its front page.

WHAT YOU GET
• The Greek text of John Burnet's Oxford edition (1901, corrected 1910) with his critical apparatus
• Benjamin Jowett's English translation (1892), a tap away on every page
• R. G. Bury's commentary (1909), section by section, with his introduction
• The syntax of every sentence, made by a parser (GLAUx) and marked as such, since it is not checked by hand
• Every word linked to its dictionary form, its inflection and the Liddell–Scott dictionaries

HOW IT WORKS
Tap a word to see its meaning and form; long press it to see the syntax of its sentence. Switch between the Greek and the English translation, and open the critical apparatus or the commentary for the page you are reading. The text is cited by the pages and sections of Stephanus (172a) with Burnet's line numbers.

Everything works offline. The text and its notes come from open data and public-domain editions, and are published openly, like the app's code, at github.com/polemistes/anagnosis.
