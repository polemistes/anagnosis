## Full description (1049)

Sophocles' Ajax in the original Greek, to read in Anagnosis, the free ancient Greek reading app. This app adds the text to Anagnosis: install the free Anagnosis app from Google Play as well, and the text appears on its front page.

WHAT YOU GET
• The Greek text of R. C. Jebb's edition (Cambridge, 1896)
• Jebb's English translation, a tap away on every page
• Jebb's critical notes and his commentary, line by line, with his introduction and appendix
• The syntax of every sentence, annotated by hand (Ancient Greek Dependency Treebank)
• Every word linked to its dictionary form, its inflection and the Liddell–Scott dictionaries

HOW IT WORKS
Tap a word to see its meaning and form; long press it to see the syntax of its sentence. Switch between the Greek and the English translation, and open the critical apparatus or the commentary for the page you are reading.

Everything works offline. The text and its notes come from open data and public-domain editions, and are published openly, like the app's code, at github.com/polemistes/anagnosis.
