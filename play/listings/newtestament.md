## Full description (1112)

The Greek New Testament, to read in Anagnosis, the free ancient Greek reading app. This app adds the text to Anagnosis: install the free Anagnosis app from Google Play as well, and the text appears on its front page.

WHAT YOU GET
• The Greek text of the SBL edition, edited by Michael W. Holmes (2010), with its apparatus of readings in other editions
• The Berean Standard Bible, a tap away on every page
• The syntax of every sentence, annotated by hand (MACULA Greek)
• Every word linked to its dictionary form, its inflection, Abbott-Smith's and Dodson's lexicons of the New Testament and the Liddell–Scott dictionaries

HOW IT WORKS
Tap a word to see its meaning and form; long press it to see the syntax of its sentence. Switch between the Greek and the English translation, and open the critical apparatus for the page you are reading. Choose the book from the menu; chapters and verses are numbered as in printed editions.

Everything works offline. The text and its notes come from open data and public-domain editions, and are published openly, like the app's code, at github.com/polemistes/anagnosis.
