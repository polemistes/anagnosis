## Πυλάδης
**Πυλάδης, -ου, ὁ** Pylades, son of Strophius king of Phocis, Orestes' friend and companion.

## Ἠλέκτρα
**Ἠλέκτρα, ἡ** Electra, daughter of Agamemnon and Clytemnestra, sister of Orestes.

## Παρνησσίς
**Παρνησσίς, -ίδος, ἡ** (adj.) of Parnassus, Parnassian (Cho. 563, the Phocian speech Orestes will put on).

## Θεστιάς
**Θεστιάς, -άδος, ἡ** daughter of Thestius: Althaea, mother of Meleager, who burned the brand on which her son's life hung (Cho. 605).

## χρυσόκμητος
**χρυσόκμητος, -ον** wrought of gold (Cho. 617, the golden necklace with which Scylla was bribed).

## Νῖσος
**Νῖσος, ὁ** Nisus, king of Megara, whose daughter Scylla cut off his immortal lock of hair for Minos (Cho. 619).

## θετοσκυθρωπός
**θετοσκυθρωπός, -όν** with a feigned sad face (Cho. 738, Sidgwick's reading).
