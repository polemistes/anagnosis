Jebb's note on the symbols of his critical apparatus:

**L** — cod. Laur. 32. 9 (first half of eleventh century).

**r** — one or more of the later MSS.: see Introd. on the text. This symbol is used where a more particular statement is unnecessary.

**MSS.** — after a reading, means that it is in all the MSS. known to the editor.

The other manuscripts the notes name are described in this volume's Introduction, in the chapter "Manuscripts, editions and commentaries" (§§ 1–4): those Jebb collated himself, the Parisian A, B, E, T, the Venetian V, V², V³, V⁴, the Bodleian Laud. Misc. 99, Laud. 54 and Barocc. 66, and the manuscript of Trinity College, Cambridge; and those he knew chiefly from earlier collations, among them Pal. (Heidelberg), Vat. a (the Vatican), M and M² (the Ambrosiana, Milan), and the Florentine L², Γ and Δ. S is the first corrector of L.
