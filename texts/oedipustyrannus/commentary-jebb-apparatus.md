## 11
στέρξαντες L 1st hand, changed by a later hand into στέξαντες: marginal gloss, ἤδη πεπονθότες. The reading στέξαντες, found in r, was intended to mean, ‘having endured,’ and may have been suggested by the glosses παθόντες, ὑπομείναντες, explaining στέρξαντες.

## 13
μὴ οὐ κατοικτείρων L: μὴ κατοικτείρων r.

## 18
ἱερεῖς MSS.: ἱερῆς Brunck: ἱερεὺς Bentley: ἱερεὺς ἔγωγε Nauck.—οἱ δέ τʼ ἠϊθέων L: the τʼ does not seem to have ever been πʼ, but may have been made from τε. οἱ δʼ ἠϊθέων r.—Dobree conj. οἱ δέ γʼ or οἵδε δʼ: Elmsley, οἱ δʼ ἔτʼ: Wecklein οἱ δʼ ἑξῆς θεῶν (‘ceteri ex ordine lecti deorum sacerdotes’). Dindorf edits οἱ δʼ ἐπʼ ᾐθέων (which Dübner believes to have been written by the 1st hand in L): and this had been conjectured by Wunder, who afterwards edited οἱ δʼ ἰηθέων, relying on a corrupt reading, οἱ δέ τʼ ἰηθέων in Suidas s. v. λεκτός.

## 21
μαντείᾳ L, made from μαντείασ: the upper part of the σ can be traced. μαντεῖα or μαντεία r.

## 29
καδμεῖον L. καδμείων r. Cp. v. 35.

## 31
οὐκ ἰσούμενον. The κ in L has been made from χ or χί.

## 35
ὅς γʼ MSS.: ὅς τʼ Elmsley, for correspondence with νῦν τʼ in v. 40.—καδμεῖον L: καδμείων r.

## 40
νῦν δʼ Blaydes.

## 43
του L, with που written over it by a late hand. που r.

## 48
πάρος L. The 1st hand wrote πάλαι, and then ροσ over λαι. The corrector deleted λαι, and wrote ροσ in the text.

## 49
μεμνώμεθα MSS.: μεμνῴμεθα Eustathius.

## 50
**στάντες τʼ**] The 1st hand in L omitted τʼ, which was added by the corrector. στάντες γʼ Triclinius.

## 67
πλάνοισ L, but altered from πλάναισ: above is written, ἀντὶ τοῦ πλάναις θηλυκῶς. πλάναις r, but with exceptions: thus T has πλάνοις (with αις written above), a marginal schol. quoting τοὺς φυγαδικοὺς πλάνους.

## 74
πέραι L. Porson conj. περᾷ, proposing to omit v. 75: see note.

## 79
προστείχοντα MSS., meaning, however, doubtless, the compound with πρός, not with πρό: cp. on O. C. 986.—προσστείχοντα Erfurdt.

## 87
τὰ δύσθροʼ is Heimsoeth's conj. suggested by the schol., λέγω γὰρ πάντα ἂν εὐτυχεῖν τὴν πόλιν, εἰ καὶ τὰ δύσφημα τύχοι [ἂν] κατʼ ὀρθὸν ἐξελθόντα. But the schol. uses that word only to illustrate his own comment on ἐσθλήν: ἀπὸ γὰρ τῶν εὐφήμων ἄρξασθαι θέλει, and clearly read δύσφορʼ, which is in the lemma of another schol.

## 88
ἐξελθόντα MSS. ἐξιόντα Suidas and Zonaras s. v. δύσφορα, probably by a mere error.

## 99
**τρόπος**] πόρος conj. F. W. Schmidt.

## 101
χειμάζον L, with ει written over ον. The ει may be from the 1st hand, as Dübner thinks: but there is room for doubting whether it was not due to the διορθωτής or first corrector (S). A, and other of the later MSS., have χειμάζον: and χειμάζει, found in a few later MSS., seems to have been merely a conjecture.

## 107
τινασ L, without accent. The scribe placed a dot over σ, to indicate that it should be deleted; but this dot was afterwards almost erased, whether by his own hand or by another. τινασ or τινὰσ r. The reading τινά seems to occur in no MS., but only in the Milan ed. of Suidas (ed. Demetrius Chalcondylas, 1498 A.D.), the other editions of Suidas giving τινάς (s. v. ἐπιστέλλει).

## 117
The 1st hand in L wrote ὅπου, which has been altered to ὅτου, perhaps by the first corrector. [I had doubted this; but in the autotype facsimile of L the original π is clear.] ὅτου r.

## 134
πρὸ τοῦ L. The 1st hand had written πρὸ στοῦ, separating the σ (as he often does) from the syllable to which it belonged, and forming στ in one character; the corrector erased the σ. Among the later MSS., A and a few more have πρὸ (sometimes with the gloss ὑπὲρ): others have πρός.—**τήνδʼ ἔθεσθʼ ἐπιστροφήν**] A variant recorded in the margin of L, τήνδε θεσπίζει γραφήν, is instructive, as indicating the lengths to which arbitrary conjecture was sometimes carried. Cp. on 1529.

## 138
αὐτοῦ L: αὑτοῦ r.

## 139
ἐκεῖνον has been made from ἐκεῖνοσ in L. The false reading ἐκεῖνος occurs in some of the later MSS.

## 159
κεκλόμενος L, with ω written over ος by a late hand. A few of the later MSS. have κεκλομένῳ or κεκλομένω.—κέκλομαι, ὦ Blaydes.—**ἄμβροτʼ**] ἄντομʼ Wecklein.

## 162
ἰὼ ἰὼ L: ἰὼ r, and Heath.

## 180
The 1st hand in L seems to have written θαναταφόρω (sic), which a later hand altered to θαναταφόρα (or θανατάφορα, for there are traces of an accent over the α). Some of the later MSS. (including A) have the dative, others the nomin.

## 182
**ἀκτὰν**] αὐδὰν Hartung, ἀχὰν Nauck.—παραβώμιον L, with most of the later MSS. (including A); some others have παρὰ βώμιον.—ἄλλαι MSS.: ἄλλαν Dindorf.

## 185
ἐπιστονάχουσι L: ἐπιστενάχουσι r.

## 191
**περιβόατος**] περιβόατον Dindorf, placing a comma after it, and reading ἀντιάζω with Hermann.

## 194
ἔπουρον, the true reading, was written by the 1st hand in L, but altered by a later hand into ἄπουρον, over which is the gloss μακράν (the prep., meaning that πάτρας ἄπουρον=‘far from our country’). The schol. knew both readings. The wrong one, ἄπουρον, prevailed in the later MSS.

## 196
**ὅρμον**] ὅρμων Döderlein.

## 198
τέλει MSS. (τέλη in Bodl. Barocc. 66, 15th cent., is doubtless a mere slip). See note.

## 200
τὸν ὦ πυρφόρων MSS. A long syllable is wanting (=v. 213 πελασθῆναι φλέγοντʼ). Hermann inserts τᾶν after ὦ: Wolff οὖν after τόν. Lachmann proposed τόν, ὦ Ζεῦ (omitting Ζεῦ in v. 202). In L a late hand has written ο over ω in πυρφόρων, and A has ει written over η in κράτη. These are traces of the reading (found in E) ὦ πυρφόρον | ἀστραπὰν κράτει νέμων.

## 205
ἀδάμαστʼ MSS.: ἀδάματʼ Erfurdt.

## 206
προσταθέντα L, with gloss προϊστάμενα. Dindorf’s conjecture, προσταχθέντα, stands in at least one late MS. (B, 15th cent.), but the rest agree with L.

## 214
ἀγλαῶπι πεύκᾳ MSS. The metrical defect (cp. v. 201) is supplied by Wolff with σύμμαχον.

## 221
αὐτὸ L: αὐτὸς r (including A).

## 227
ὑπεξελὼν | αὐτὸς MSS. I read ὑπεξελεῖν (already proposed by K. Halm and Blaydes) αὐτόν.

## 229
ἀσφαλής L, with γρ. ἀβλαβής in margin. Most of the later MSS. (including A) have ἀβλαβής, which is the reading of the Aldine, Brunck, Hermann, Elmsley, Linwood, Wunder, Blaydes, Kennedy: while among the editors who prefer ἀσφαλής are Schneidewin, Nauck, Dindorf (with the admission, ‘hic tamen aptius videtur ἀβλαβής’), Wecklein, Wolff, Tournier, Campbell, White.

## 230
**ἐξ ἄλλης χθονὸς**] For ἐξ, Vauvilliers conj. ἢ ʼξ: Seyffert, ἐξ ἀμῆς: but see note.

## 239
**μήτε θύμασιν**] μηδὲ θύμασιν Elmsley.

## 240
χέρνιβοσ was written by the 1st hand in L (and occurs in at least one later MS., L², cod. Laur. 31. 10), but was changed by an early hand to χέρνιβασ, which is in almost all the later MSS.

## 248
κακὸν κακῶσ νιν κἄμοιρον ἐκτρίψαι βίον L 1st hand: the κ before ἄμοιρον was afterwards erased. One of the later MSS. (B) has κἄμοιρον, and all seem to have ἄμοιρον. ἄμορον Porson.

## 257
**βασιλέως τʼ**] The 1st hand in L had joined the στ in one character (cp. on v. 134). An early hand (perhaps that of the first corrector) afterwards erased the τʼ, and then wrote it separately from the σ. Some later MSS. omit the τʼ.

## 258
κυρῶ τʼ MSS.: κυρῶ γʼ T. F. Benedict (Observationes in Soph., Lips., 1820: cp. Blaydes ad loc.).

## 260
**ἔχων δὲ**] ἔχω δὲ L 1st hand; an early hand added ν.

## 261
**κοινῶν τε**] καὶ νῷν τὰ M. Schmidt.

## 270
γῆν L: γῆς Vauvilliers.

## 273
τοῖς τʼ ἄλλοισι Jernstedt: τοῖς ἄλλοισι Καδμείοις θʼ F. W. Schmidt.

## 281
ἃν Brunck; the MSS. have ἀν (as L), or ἂν.

## 290
τά τʼ L: τά γʼ r (including A, where the 1st hand had begun to write τὰ δʼ).

## 293
τὸν δʼ ἰδόντʼ MSS. τὸν δὲ δρῶντʼ is an anonymous conjecture cited by Burton.

## 294
The 1st hand in L wrote δείματοστʼ, (there is no trace of an accent on ο,) joining στ in one character; the corrector afterwards wrote τʼ separately, as in 134, 257. (The facsimile shows that this τʼ was not made from γʼ.) δείματός τʼ was the reading of almost all the later MSS.: indeed, it does not appear certain that any one of them has γʼ.—δειμάτων ἔχει Hartung.

## 297
The 1st hand in L wrote οὑξελλέγχων: the first λ has been erased, and -ξων written above, either by the 1st hand itself (as Dübner thinks), or by the first corrector. The later MSS. are divided between οὑξελέγξων and οὑξελέγχων: A supports the former, which, on the whole, has the advantage in authority, and is also recommended by Greek usage: see comm.

## 305
εἰ καὶ μὴ MSS.: εἴ τι μὴ L. Stephani: εἰ μὴ καὶ F. V. Fritzsch.

## 307
**τοῦδε**] τήνδε Blaydes.

## 308
**εὖ**] ἢ Meineke.

## 310
**σύ νυν**] The 1st hand in L seems to have written σὺ νῦν, which a later hand changed to σὺ δʼ οὖν. (I formerly thought that the 1st hand had written σὺ οὖν, omitting δʼ.) σὺ δʼ οὖν r.

## 315
ἔχοι L: ἔχει r.—πόνοσ L, with ων written above οσ by the first corrector (S). Several of the later MSS. (including A) have πόνων, though πόνος continued to be current as a variant.

## 317
λύηι L: λύει or λύη r.

## 322
ἔννο μʼ L, with an erasure between ο and μʼ. The 1st hand had written ἔννομον (found in some later MSS.); the correction may be due either to the 1st hand itself, or to the διορθωτής (S). L has προσφιλῆ, with ες written above, by S (I think), rather than by the 1st hand. Many later MSS. (including A) combine ἔννομʼ with προσφιλὲς, though the latter error was prob. generated by ἔννομον.

## 325
**μηδʼ ἐγὼ**] μὴ λέγων Kvíčala.

## 326
L rightly assigns these two verses to Oedipus. Several later MSS. give them to the Chorus, probably because v. 327 was thought less suitable to the person of the king. But there is no fitting place for the interposition of the Chorus before v. 404.

## 332
ἐγώ τʼ L (with οὔτε written over ἐμαυτόν): ἐγὼ οὔτε r.

## 336
κἀπαραίτητος Sehrwald.

## 337
ὁρμὴν L 1st hand. γ has been written over μ by an early hand (prob. S), which has also sought to make μ into γ in the text. ὀργὴν r.—τὴν σὴν δʼ L, and so almost all the later MSS. But one at least (V⁴) has τὴν σοὶ δʼ, which Dindorf adopts.

## 347
εἰργάσθαι δʼ L 1st hand, but the δʼ has been re-touched, to make θʼ. εἰργάσθαι θʼ r.

## 360
L has ἢ ʼκπειρᾶι λέγειν, with ο written under the accent on λεγ, and a mark of abbreviation, /\, over ειν. Dübner thinks that the 1st hand wrote λέγ, denoting ειν by the mark aforesaid, and indicating by ο a reading λόγων, to which a marginal gloss by a later hand refers, εἰ πεῖραν λόγων κινεῖς: then another hand wrote ειν in full. Campbell holds that the 1st hand wrote λέγοι. All the later MSS. have λέγειν; and I believe, with Dübner, that this was what the 1st hand in L meant to give. The superscript ο, however, is not (I think) from the first hand, but from a later one, prob. the same that wrote the marg. gloss. The ειν may be from the first corrector (S).—Hartung reads ἢ πειρᾷ λέγων; Campbell, ἢ ʼκπειρᾷ λόγῳ; Wecklein and Bellermann, ἢ ἐκπειρᾷ λόγων; Blaydes proposes οὐχὶ ξυνῆκας; πρὸς τί μου ʼκπειρᾷ λέγειν; Mekler, ἢ πέτρᾳ ʼλεγον; F. W. Schmidt, ἢ ἑτέρᾳ λέγω;

## 374
**μιᾶς**] μαίας G. Wolff.

## 376
με μοῖρα πρός γε σοῦ L (and so the later MSS., except that Δ has σε...γε σοῦ): σε μοῖρα πρός γʼ ἐμοῦ Brunck.

## 379
Κρέων δέ MSS.: Κρέων γε Brunck.

## 396
τοῦ L, του r.

## 405
Οἰδίπου. L and the other MSS. support this form of the voc. here, and in O. C. 557, 1346; but Οἰδίπους (voc.) in twelve other places. Elmsley and Reisig, whom Dindorf follows, hold Οἰδίπους to be alone correct. Here, at least, euphony recommends Οἰδίπου. It is more probable that both forms were admissible.

## 413
δέδορκασ καὶ L. δεδορκὼς κοὐ r.

## 420
**λιμὴν**] μυχὸς Wecklein.

## 434
σχολῇ σʼ MSS.: σχολῇ γʼ Suidas, and so Porson, inserting σʼ after ἐμούς.

## 438
**ἥδʼ ἡμέρα φύσει σε**] τῇδʼ ἡμέρᾳ πεύσει σφε Nauck.

## 439
ἄγαντʼ L 1st hand: the τʼ has been erased.

## 445
**σύ γʼ ἐμποδὼν**] L has σύγʼ in an erasure. The 1st hand seems to have written ταῦτʼ: an early corrector (S?) wrote γρ. σύ γε in the margin, and altered the word in the text. One later MS. (Vat. a) has σύμʼ; another (B) τά γʼ.

## 446
ἀλγύναισ L: ἀλγύνοις Elmsley.

## 461
λάβῃσ ἐψευσμένον L: λάβῃς μʼ ἐψευσμένον r, which Brunck and Hermann preferred. Blaydes suggests that, with λάβῃς μʼ, ἔμʼ ἤδη might be changed to τότʼ ἤδη. Wilamowitz conj. λάβῃς ἐψευσμένα.

## 463
εἶπε L. The letters ει (written [ligature]) are in an erasure, which would have been unnecessary if the word first written had been εἶδε: it seems to have been ἥδε. In one of the later MSS. (Γ) the 1st hand wrote εἶδε, which has been corrected to εἶπε. The Scholiast knew both readings: but it is hardly doubtful that εἶδε was a conjecture or a corruption.

## 466
ἀελλοπόδων MSS.; ἀελλάδων Hesychius.

## 472
κῆρεσ has been made from χεῖρεσ in L.—ἀναπλάκητοι L, with μ written above the second α. The false reading ἀναμπλάκητοι is found in most (but not all) later MSS. In T there is a Triclinian note, ἀναπλάκητοι γὰρ γράφειν (on metrical grounds)…εὕρηται γὰρ καὶ ἔν τινι τῶν παλαιοτάτων βιβλίων.

## 478
L now has πέτρα σ ὡσ ταῦροσ, with an erasure between α and σ, and traces of correction at ὡσ τ. The 1st hand had written πετραῖοσ ὁ ταῦροσ: the correction is old, perh. by the first corrector (S). Most of the later MSS. have πέτρας ὡς ταῦρος: one or two, πετραῖος ὡς ταῦρος.—J. F. Martin, and (later, but independently) E. L. Lushington, conjectured πέτρας ἰσόταυρος: M. Schmidt, πέτρας ἴσα ταύροις: Dorville, πέτρας ἅτε ταῦρος: Campbell, πέτραισιν ἔναυλος.

## 483
**δεινὰ μὲν οὖν**] δεινά με νῦν Bergk: δεινά με νοῦν Nauck.

## 493
There is a defect in the text as given by L and the other MSS., the antistrophic verse (508) being φανερὰ γὰρ ἐπʼ αὐτῷ πτερόεσσʼ ἦλθε κόρα. (See Metrical Analysis.) The alternatives are, (1) to supply ⏑ ⏑ – – after ἔμαθον, or after ὅτου δὴ: (2) to supply – ⏑ ⏑ – after βασάνῳ. It may be noticed that in L the words πρὸσ ὅτου δὴ stand in a line by themselves, the large space left after them suggesting the loss of something there. See comment.—One later MS. (Bodl. Laud. 54) has παρʼ ὅτου, with the gloss παρʼ οὗ, ἤγουν τοῦ νείκους.

## 508
**φανερὰ γὰρ ἐπʼ αὐτῷ**] Hermann, thinking v. 493 (ἔμαθον κ.τ.λ.) to be complete as it stands in the MSS., omitted the words γὰρ ἐπʼ αὐτῷ in his first ed. (though he afterwards replaced them); and Dindorf did likewise. Triclinius omitted ἐπʼ αὐτῷ, merely on the ground that he thought them unsuitable, but retained γάρ.

## 510
ἡδύπολις MSS.: ἁδύπολις Erfurdt and Dindorf.

## 516
πρόσ τʼ ἐμοῦ L, with traces of erasure at τʼ and ἐ. The 1st hand had written πρόστεμοῦ (or possibly πρόσγεμοῦ), joining σ, as so often, to the following letter: the corrector erased the τ (or γ), and wrote τʼ separately (cp. 134, 257, 294).—πρός γʼ ἐμοῦ r, and Suidas (s.v. βάξιν).—πρός τι μου Hartung. This was an old conjecture: τι is written above the line in L, and in several of the later MSS. It may have been a result, rather than cause, of the false reading πρός τʼ.

## 517
ἔργοισί τι βλάβην φέρον Kennedy.

## 525
τοῦ πρόσ δʼ L. Of the later MSS. some (as B) have τοῦ πρὸς δʼ: others (as A) πρὸς τοῦδʼ (not τοῦ δʼ): others (as Γ and L²) τοὖπος or τοῦπος.—τοὔπος is read by most of the recent edd.: see comment.

## 528
ἐξ ὀμμάτων ὀρθῶν δὲ L (the δὲ having been made from τε by a later hand). Most of the later MSS. have either this, or (as A) ἐξ ὀμμάτων ὀρθῶν τε. The reading which seems preferable, ἐξ ὀμμάτων δʼ ὀρθῶν τε, is given by Suidas and a few later MSS. (Γ, Δ, Trin.).

## 537
ἐν ἐμοὶ MSS.: ἔν μοι Reisig.

## 538
γνωρίσοιμι MSS.: γνωριοῖμι Elmsley.

## 539
ἢ οὐκ A. Spengel: κοὐκ MSS.

## 541
πλήθους MSS. The conjecture πλούτου, first made by an anonymous German translator of the play in 1803, has been adopted by Nauck and others.

## 546
**ηὕρηκʼ**] εὕρηκʼ L. See comment. Cp. 1051.

## 555
χρείη Dawes. L has χρεῖʼ ἦ, but the accentuation is due to the first corrector, and the ῏ over η has been re-touched by a later hand. The 1st hand may have intended χρειη or χρείη, though the space between ει and η is rather unduly wide. χρεῖʼ ἦ is in almost all the later MSS. (χρεῖʼ ἦν Γ; χρείμʼ Bodl. Barocc. 66, with α superscript). Cp. v. 791.

## 561
ἀναμετρηθεῖεν A, a reading which no other MS. seems to have. Cp. 1348, where ἂν γνῶναι has been changed to ἀναγνῶναι in all the MSS.

## 566
**θανόντος**] κτανόντος Meineke: θενόντος M. Schmidt.

## 567
**κοὐκ ἠκούσαμεν**] κοὐκ ἰχνεύσαμεν Mekler: κοὐδὲν ἤνομεν Nauck.

## 570
**τοσόνδε γʼ**] τὸ σὸν δέ L 1st hand: the corrector changed σὸν to σόν, as if to indicate the reading τοσόνδε. τοσόνδε is in a few of the later MSS. (as B, with gl. τοσοῦτον): τὸ σὸν δέ in A and others.—τὸ σὸν δέ γʼ is read by Brunck, and others: τοσόνδε γʼ by Porson (Eur. Med. 461), Elmsley, and others. The reading τόσον δέ γʼ, already known to Triclinius, and also suggested by Reisig, is preferred by Wunder and others.

## 572
τὰς MSS.: τάσδʼ Döderlein.

## 575
ταῦθʼ MSS.: ταὔθʼ Brunck.

## 579
Wecklein writes τῆς τιμῆς instead of γῆς ἴσον: Heimsoeth conjectures τοῦ κράτους for ταὐτὰ γῆς: F. W. Schmidt, ἀρχῆς δʼ ἐκείνῃ ταῦτʼ ἔχεις ἴσον νέμων.

## 583
**ἐγὼ**] ἔχω is Heimsoeth's conjecture, who might point to v. 1061, where ἐγὼ is right, and the MSS. give ἔχω.

## 597
ἐκκαλοῦσι L, with a gloss προκαλοῦσιν written above. There is no trace of a variant in the later MSS., for in E καλοῦσι is a mere blunder, and the παρα written in the margin of L and A was meant to explain ἐκ, not to suggest a v. l. παρακαλοῦσι. That ἐκκαλοῦσι was rightly understood, appears from such glosses as μεσ[ίτην] ποιοῦσι (B), εἰς βοήθειαν μεσοῦντα (E).—αἰκάλλουσι Musgrave.

## 598
τὸ γὰρ τυχεῖν αὐτοῖσ ἅπαν ἐνταῦθʼ ἔνι L. The accent on αὐτοῖσ has been either made or re-touched by the first corrector (S); Dübner and Campbell think that the 1st hand wrote αὐτοὺσ. This is possible, but seems hardly certain. They also find traces of τ, written by an early hand after ἅπαν, but now erased. Of the later MSS., a few have ἅπαν, the majority (as A) ἅπαντʼ, but two (Γ and L²) the probably true reading, πᾶν.—πάντʼ is read by Bothe and Burges.—Wecklein brackets the verse as spurious.

## 602
**δρῶντος**] δρῶν τόσʼ Bellermann; δρῶν τόδʼ Förster.

## 604
πεύθου L, the letters πευ in an erasure; the 1st hand perh. wrote ἐπύθου, as Dübner thinks. πεύθου prevails in the later MSS., but Γ has πύθου, and Pal. πυθοῦ. Nauck prefers πυθοῦ, as Dindorf did in Poet. Scen. ed. 5 (1869).

## 608
Bellermann conject. γνώμης δὲ δήλου.

## 623
θνήισκειν L. See comment. on 118.

## 624
ὡς ἂν is my conjecture for ὅταν. The MSS. give v. 624 to Creon, and v. 625 to Oedipus. After v. 625 a verse seems to be lost.

## 629
ἄρχοντοσ L, made from ἄρχοντεσ either by the first hand or by the first corrector (S).—ἄρχοντας Musgrave.

## 631
**καιρίαν**] κυρίαν L, the υ in an erasure of two letters, of which the second was ι: in the margin, γρ. καιρίαν. Most of the later MSS. have καιρίαν.

## 634

## 800
This verse does not stand in the text of L, but has been added in the margin by a later hand. With regard to the age of the hand, Mr E. M. Thompson observes:—‘This writing is of the style which appears in the latter part of the thirteenth century, and continues with little variation for some fifty years or more. ## 814
Λαίῳ MSS.: Λαΐου Bothe. Blaydes suggests, εἰ δέ τι ξένῳ | τούτῳ προσήκει Λαίῳ τε συγγενές: Heimsoeth, εἰ δὲ τῷ ξένῳ | τούτῳ προσῆν καὶ Λαΐῳ τι συγγενές.

## 815
τίς τοῦδέ γʼ ἀνδρὸσ νῦν ἔστʼ ἀθλιώτεροσ L. The νῦν is almost erased, and over it a late hand has written ἄλλωσ, probably meant for ἄλλοσ. The later MSS. either agree with L, or give τίς τοῦδέ γʼ ἀνδρός ἐστιν ἀθλιώτερος (as A). Kennedy adopts the latter, and so Campbell (with τἀνδρὸς for γʼ ἀνδρὸς). But νῦν seems forcible here. Dindorf proposed νῦν ἔτʼ (which Wecklein receives); he afterwards wrote τίς τοῦδʼ ἀκούειν ἀνδρὸς ἀθλιώτερος: but now rejects the verse. Bellermann writes νῦν ἂν (to go with γένοιτʼ). I would merely transpose ἀνδρὸς and omit γʼ, which might easily have been intruded, for metre’s sake, when the proper order of words had been deranged.

## 817
ᾧ...τινα L. Schaefer wrote ὃν..τινα (so that ἔξεστι should be absolute): Dindorf, ὃν ..τινι. Nauck proposes εἰ μὴ ξένων.. τινι | .. προσφωνεῖν ἐμέ (instead of τινα).

## 824
μἤστι. The 1st hand in L wrote μήστι, which an early hand changed to μήτε. The latter is in most of the later MSS. (with γρ. μή ʼστι in some, as T).

## 825
**μηδʼ ἐμβατεύειν**] L has μήτʼ, made by an early hand from μἤστ, as Campbell thinks, and as seems most probable; or, as Dübner thinks, from μή μʼ. Dindorf’s μηδʼ is clearly right. The alternatives would be to read μἤστι τοὺς ἐμοὺς ἰδεῖν, | μἤστʼ ἐμβατεύειν, which does not seem Sophoclean, or μήτε...μήτʼ, supplying ἔξεστι (as Elmsley suggested), which is much worse.

## 827

## 840
πάθος MSS.: ἄγος has been conjectured by Arndt, Blaydes, and M. Schmidt.

## 843
L has κατακτείναιεν, but the letters αι are in an erasure, having been made by an early corrector. Wolff thinks that the 1st hand wrote κατακτείνοιεν. As the last ε is certainly from the 1st hand, the 1st hand must have written either that or κατακτείνειεν, which is in at least one later MS. (Pal.), others having κατακτείναιεν (as A), or κατακτείνειαν. Most of the recent edd. give κατακτείνειαν. It is perhaps safest to do so, in the absence of better evidence for -αιεν (or -οιεν) than we have in this passage. Yet cp. the inscription in Kaibel’s Epigrammata (24. 2), ἐχθροὶ στήσαιεν Ζηνὶ τρόπαιον ἕδος (date, circ. 400—350 B.C.); to which Meisterhans (Gramm. der Attischen Inschriften, p. 75) refers in proof that ‘the poets of the 4th cent. B.C. could use, without metrical necessity, the un-Attic forms of the aorist optative.’

## 876
ἀκροτάταν εἰσαναβᾶσʼ ἀπότομον | ὤρουσεν εἰσ ἀνάγκαν L. All MSS. have ἀκροτάταν. Instead of ἀπότομον, A has ἄποτμον, with ο written above.—ἀκρότατα γεῖσʼ ἀναβᾶσʼ Wolff; ἀποτμοτάταν (for ἀπότομον) Schnelle. See comment.

## 890
ἔρξεται L. The scribe had begun to write χ as the third letter, but corrected it to ξ. The later MSS. have the same word, with variations of breathing.

## 891

## 892

## 896
After χορεύειν, L has in the same verse πονεῖν ἢ τοῖσ θεοῖσ. These words are found in at least four other MSS.,—Pal., M (as corrected), M², M⁵: being a corruption of a gloss, πανηγυρίζειν τοῖς θεοῖς, found in the Trin. and other MSS. (Campbell, 1. xxvii). Dr E. M. Thompson points out that this corruption, hardly possible in uncial writing, would have been comparatively easy in minuscule, and regards it as indicating that the archetype of L was a minuscule MS. (Introd. to Facsimile, p. 8.)

## 1084

## 1264
L has πλεκταῖσ ἐώραισ (corrected from ἐωραῖσ) ἐμπεπλεγμένην (from ἐμπεπληγμένην)· ὁ δὲ | ὅπως δʼ ὁρᾷ νιν. The poet prob. wrote πλεκταῖσιν αἰώραισιν ἐμπεπλεγμένην· | ὁ δʼ ὡς ὁρᾷ νιν. Then (1) αἰώραισιν became αἰώραις, which is found in some later MSS. (as B, V): (2) αἰώραις was changed for metre’s sake to ἐώραις, as it is in L, A, and others: (3) to complete v. 1264, now too short by a foot, the words ὁ δὲ were borrowed from ὁ δʼ ὡς at the beginning of 1265: and (4) ὡς in 1265 became the metrically requisite ὅπως. The δʼ after ὅπως in L may be a survival from the original ὁ δʼ ὡς. A has ὁ δὲ | ὅπως without δʼ. Wecklein reads as I do, but with ὅπως δʼ instead of ὁ δʼ ὡς. We seem, however, to need the pron. here. The case would thus resemble that of vv. 943, 944,—a gap in the former verse being filled with words borrowed from the latter, which was afterwards expanded.—Nauck conjectures πλεκταῖσιν ἀρτάναισιν αἰωρουμένην.

## 1383

## 1387

## 1388

The original form of the verb was κληΐω (being formed from the noun-stem κλῆϝι, cp. κονίω, μηνίω), and κλῄω, not κλείω, was the older Attic form, still used, doubtless, in the time of Sophocles: thus κληίς occurs in an Attic inscription later than 403 B.C.; though κλείς, κλεῖθρον, etc., occur as early as about 378—330 B.C. (Meisterhans, Gramm. Att. Inschr. p. 17.) The spelling of κλείω, etc., fluctuates in our MSS.: thus L has κλεῖθρα above in v. 1262, but κλῆιθρα in 1287, 1294.

Here, as in most cases, either pres. or aor. is admissible; but the aor. seems clearly preferable.

κρέων L: κρέον r. Cp. on 637.

## 1460
πρόσθῃ (sic) L, with most of the later MSS. The ancient grammarians were not agreed on the accentuation of such forms; cp. Chandler, Greek Accentuation, § 820, 2nd ed. In Her. 6. 109 MSS. give προσθῇ. Elmsley conjectured προθῇ (V has πρόθη).

## 1462
τοῖν...οἷν. Attic inscriptions of the 5th and 4th cent. B.C. recognise no dual in -α, -αιν for pronoun-forms in -α, -η. Thus they give, as fem., τώ, τοῖν, τούτοιν, οἷν. See Meisterhans, Gr. d. Att. Inschr. p. 50.

## 1466
**οἷν**] Heath’s emendation ταῖν is received by Brunck, Erfurdt, and others. I found ταῖν in one of the later MSS., V², and Blaydes cites it from cod. Paris. 2820, with gloss τούτων: it was probably an old conjecture, intended to smooth the construction. See comment. on 1462 ff.

## 1470
σφᾶσ L, though the ᾶ might easily be taken for ὰ, the accent found in some later MSS.

## 1474
ἐγγόνοιν L; ἐκγόνοιν r (B, V⁴).

## 1477
ἥ σʼ εἶχεν L: ἣν εἶχες r (including A), evidently a prosaic correction. Wunder, whom Hermann and others follow, adopts ἥ σʼ ἔχει from one 14th century MS. (Laur. 32. 2), taking πάλαι with γνούς. For παροῦσαν Kvíčala conjectures πάρος σὴν, Blaydes πάροιθε.

## 1481
ὡς MSS.: εἰς Elmsley.

## 1487
**τὰ λοιπὰ τοῦ πικροῦ**] Some of the later MSS. have τὰ πικρὰ τοῦ λοιποῦ, which Blaydes prefers, because hitherto their lives had not been bitter. This may have been the motive of the change, unless it was a mere oversight: but L’s reading is equivalent to τὸν λοιπὸν βίον τὸν πικρόν.

## 1491
**ἵξεσθʼ**] ἥξεθʼ L 1st hand: an early corrector (the first, S, acc. to Dübner) changed this to ἵξεθʼ, writing σ above the ε, i.e. ἵξεσθʼ. Some of the later MSS. (B, E, V⁴) have ἥξετʼ, generated, doubtless, by ἥξετʼ in 1489: as conversely in 1489 T has ἵξετʼ, prompted by ἵξεσθʼ here.

## 1493
**ἔσται, τίς**] Elmsley conjectured ἐστιν ὃς (one of the later MSS., E, has ἔσται γʼ ὅς): ‘at languet hoc,’ as Hermann says.

## 1494
τοῖς ἐμοῖς | γονεῦσιν MSS. Schenkel conjectures γόνοισιν: Arndt, γαμβροῖσιν: Kennedy ταῖς ἐμαῖς | γοναῖσιν. Hartung changes ἐμοῖς to γάμοις, and δηλήματα to ʼκμεμαγμένα (‘reproaches which will cleave to your marriage, on your parents’ account and on your own’). Heimsoeth would keep γονεῦσιν, and change ἃ τοῖς ἐμοῖς to ἃ ʼκ τῆς ἴσης.

## 1497
Nauck supposes that Soph. wrote, after ἔπεφνεν, merely οὗπερ αὐτὸς ἐσπάρη, | κἀκτήσαθʼ ὑμᾶς ὧνπερ αὐτὸς ἐξέφυ. He now grants that ὅθεν can mean ἐξ ἧς, but objects to τῶν ἴσων, and to the marriage being dwelt upon at more length than the parricide.

## 1505
μή σφε παρίδῃς MSS. (παρίδησ L). Dawes conjectured μή σφε περιίδῃς: Fritzsch, μὴ περί σφʼ ἴδῃς: μὴ παρά σφʼ ἴδῃς Porson: Erfurdt, μή σφε δὴ (μοι Blaydes) προδῷς, and afterwards μή σφʼ ἀτιμάσῃς.

## 1506
ἐγγενεῖς MSS. (made in L from ἐνγενεῖσ). Dindorf conjectures ἐκγενεῖς, comparing ἔκβιος, ἔκτιμος, ἐξούσιος: Hermann, ἀστέγους: Schneidewin, ἐκστεγεῖς: Wolff, συγγενής.

## 1511
εἰχέτην MSS.: εἴχετόν γʼ Brunck.

## 1512
εὔχεσθέ μοι MSS. (In L the third ε had been αι.)—Wunder, εὔχεσθʼ ἐμοί: Blaydes, τοῦθʼ ἓν εὔχομαι (so Wecklein), suggesting also τοῦτʼ ἐπεύχομαι: Dindorf, ηὔχθω μόνον. (Plat. Phaedr. 279 C has ηὖκται, pass., and Soph. Tr. 610 ηὔγμην, midd.: but the imperat. of ηὖγμαι does not occur.)

## 1513
οὗ καιρὸς ἀεὶ ζῆν τοῦ βίου δὲ λῴονος MSS. The modes of correction tried have been chiefly three. (1) Omitting ζῆν, Elmsley explains thus: εὔχεσθε κυρῆσαι τοῦ βίου οὗ καιρὸς ἀεὶ (κυρῆσαί ἐστι), λῴονος δὲ τοῦ φυτ. πατρός. Hermann, also omitting ζῆν, makes εὔχεσθε passive (i.e. ‘let that prayer be made for you by me, which is fitting at each season’). (2) Omitting τοῦ, Hartung writes, οὗ καιρός, αἰεὶ ζῆν, βίου δὲ λῴονος. Blaydes and Campbell read thus, but keep ἀεί, and place no comma after καιρός. (3) Others alter ἀεί. Dindorf gives οὗ καιρὸς ἐᾷ ζῆν, τοῦ βίου δὲ λῴονος. This has been the most generally received emendation, and seems the best. Meineke, οὗ καιρὸς ᾖ ζῆν: Blaydes, οὗ καιρός, εὖ ζῆν.

## 1517
εἰμί L: εἶμι Brunck.

## 1518
πέμψεισ L 1st hand, corrected to πέμψηισ, and then (by a still later hand) back to πέμψεισ. The later MSS. are divided, but most have πέμψεις.—ἀπʼ οἴκων L, ου written over ων by a late hand. Most of the later MSS. have ἀπʼ οἴκων (over which in A is γρ. ἄποικον), but V² has ἀποίκων, and B ἄποικον.

## 1521
νῦν (bis) L, and so Wolff; νυν (bis) Brunck, and most edd. T has νῦν ..νυν, but this, at least, can hardly be right, though νυν ..νῦν would be quite defensible.

## 1523
**τῷ βίῳ**] διὰ βίου Nauck.

## 1524-1530
The MSS. rightly give these verses to the Chorus. The Scholiast gives them to Oedipus, but thinks that the play would end better with v. 1523: τὰ γὰρ ἑξῆς ἀνοίκεια, γνωμολογοῦντος τοῦ Οἰδίποδος. This error arose, as Dindorf points out, from the fact that in Eur. Phoen. 1758 ff. Oed. speaks similar verses, of which the first two are taken almost verbatim from our passage:—ὦ πάτρας κλεινῆς πολῖται, λεύσσετʼ, Οἰδίπους ὅδε, | ὃς τὰ κλείνʼ αἰνίγματʼ ἔγνω καὶ μέγιστος ἦν ἀνήρ.—Fr. Ritter would delete vv. 1524—1530: but the close of the play would then be too abrupt.

## 1526
ὅστισ οὐ ζήλῳ πολιτῶν καὶ τύχαισ ἐπιβλέπων L. In the later MSS. the only variations are ἐν for οὐ (V, M, M⁵ 1st hand), and βίῳ for ζήλῳ (M),—mere blunders. Musgrave conjectured, ὃν τίς οὐ ζήλῳ πολιτῶν τῆς τύχης ἐπέβλεπεν; (So Blaydes.) Combining ἐπέβλεπεν with two other conjectures (Martin’s οὗ τις, and Ellendt’s ταῖς for καὶ) Hartung restored, οὗ τίς οὐ ζήλῳ πολιτῶν ταῖς τύχαις ἐπέβλεπεν. Nauck now reads, οὗ τίς οὐ ζήλῳ πολιτῶν ἦν τύχαις ἐπιβλέπων (ἦν for καὶ with Enger). Campbell conjectures πρῶτος ἐν ζήλῳ πολιτῶν καὶ τύχαις ἐπιφλέγων, citing a gloss ἐπαιρόμενος (on ἐπιβλέπων) which occurs in M (not, however, in E, where on p. 110, which contains vv. 1518—1530, there is no gloss).

## 1528
**ἐκείνην**] κείνην L 1st hand: the initial ε is from the first corrector (S).—ἰδεῖν has been suspected: see comment on 1529.

## 1529
In L four words (probably belonging to a gloss) have been erased above μηδένʼ ὀλβίζειν πρὶν ἄν. In the margin the first corrector has written γρ. πάντα προσδοκᾶν ἕως ἄν: i.e., some copies had πάντα προσδοκᾶν ἕως (to which the corrector of L has wrongly added ἄν) for μηδένʼ ὀλβίζειν πρὶν ἄν,—a conjecture of the same class as that noticed on v. 134.
