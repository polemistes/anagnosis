Hude's critical apparatus stands at the foot of each page of his text, in Latin. In the print each item begins with the number of the line of the page it concerns; the app leaves these numbers out and files each item under the section that holds the word it speaks of. An item gives the reading of the text, then who has it, then after a colon the other readings ("εἴητε marg. Ald.: εἰ ἦτε C H X"); an item without a colon reports a variant or a conjecture that Hude does not adopt ("ἀποδεῖξαι Herwerden").

## Speeches 1–31

**X** — codex Palatinus 88 (Heidelberg). According to Hude's Praefatio it was written in the twelfth century and is the source of all the other manuscripts of speeches 3–31; for speeches 1 and 2 other manuscripts are independent witnesses beside it. Its hands are told apart:

* **X¹** — the reading before a correction was made
* **Xᶜ** — the reading brought in by a correction
* **Xᶜ (1)** — the reading brought in by a correction of the first hand
* **Xʳ** — the reading brought in by a correction of a later hand
* **Xˢ** — a reading written above the line by the first hand

The same raised letters and figures are used with the other manuscripts (H¹, Hᶜ, Mᶜ, P¹).

**C** — codex Laurentianus lvii, 4 · **D** — Laurentianus lvii, 45 · **E** — Laurentianus lvii, 52 · **F** — Marcianus 416 · **f** — Vaticanus 69 · **G** — Marcianus 417 · **g** — Laurentianus lxxxvi, 13 · **H** — Marcianus 422 · **h** — Marcianus app. cl. x, 15 · **I** — Marcianus 522 · **K** — Marcianus app. cl. viii, 1 · **M** — Vaticanus 66 · **N** — Vaticanus 1366 · **O** — Urbinas 117 · **P** — Vaticanus Palatinus 117 · **Q** — Vaticanus 894 · **q** — Laurentianus iv, 33 · **R** — Vaticanus Urbinas 131 · **S** — Sangermanensis · **U** — Parisinus 3017 · **V** — Parisinus Coislinianus 249 · **W** — Vindobonensis

Capital and small letters are different manuscripts (F and f, G and g, H and h, Q and q).

**L** — the agreement of the manuscripts (above all of X and C)

**Aldus** — the Aldine edition · **marg. Ald.** — the margin of the Leiden copy of the Aldine edition · **Turr.** — the Zürich editors (Baiter and Sauppe)

**+** — an erased letter · **/** — the space of one letter · **[X]** — the reading of X is lacking or uncertain

## Speeches 32–34

These three speeches survive only as quotations in Dionysius of Halicarnassus; the sigla are those of his manuscripts.

**F** — codex Laurentianus lix, 15 · **G** — Guelferbytanus 806 · **M** — Ambrosianus D 119 sup. · **T** — Parisinus 2944 · **L** — the agreement of the manuscripts

## Words and abbreviations of the apparatus

Hude gives no key to these; they are the usual ones of a Latin apparatus.

add. (*addidit*) added · om. (*omisit*) left out · del. (*delevit*) deleted · corr. corrected · suppl. supplied · transp. transposed · coni. conjectured · lac. / lacunam statuit: marks a gap · suprascr., supra v. written above (the line) · in ras. over an erasure · comp. in an abbreviation · marg. in the margin · rell. (*reliqui*) the other manuscripts · all. (*alii*) others · soli alone · alt. the second (of two like words in the line) · pr. the first · it. (*item*) likewise · vulg. the common text · cf. compare.

In the text, square brackets [ ] enclose words Hude judges not to be Lysias's, angle brackets ⟨ ⟩ words added by conjecture, and three dots a gap in the transmitted text.
