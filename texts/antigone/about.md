The Greek text of Sir Richard Jebb's edition, with his critical apparatus and commentary, his translation in the modernised wording of the Perseus Digital Library, and the Liddell–Scott dictionaries. Tap any word for its meaning and form. The syntax of the sentences is annotated by hand; the syntax trees were made on the Perseus Digital Library's text of the play as the treebank's annotators had it, which differs in a few words from the text printed here, and where a sentence differs the syntax view shows both and marks the words. About two words in a hundred are not analysed as the treebank has them: where the printed text differs from the treebank's, the dictionary form and grammatical form were set by an automatic analyser, by rule or by an AI model.

The Greek text, the critical apparatus and the commentary were read from a scan of the printed edition by OCR and checked against the page images by AI models, in two readings. The English translation is not Jebb's printed wording: it is the Perseus Digital Library's digital text, which puts his translation into modern English. Jebb's introduction is the Perseus Digital Library's digital text of the third edition (1900).
