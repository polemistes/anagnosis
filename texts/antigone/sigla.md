# title: Manuscripts and sigla
# source: R. C. Jebb, Sophocles: The Plays and Fragments, Part III: The Antigone, 2nd ed., Cambridge 1891, Introduction § "Manuscripts" and the preface to the critical notes
# license: CC BY-SA 4.0

Jebb's critical notes report the readings of the following manuscripts. For L he used the Autotype Facsimile published by the London Hellenic Society in 1885, and aimed to make the report of that manuscript complete and exact.

**L** — cod. Laur. 32. 9 (Florence, Laurentian Library), first half of the eleventh century. The principal manuscript.

**A** — thirteenth century.

**E** — ascribed to the thirteenth century, but perhaps of the fourteenth.

**T** — fifteenth century.

**V** — late thirteenth or early fourteenth century.

**V2** — probably fourteenth century.

**V3, V4, Vat., Vat. b, L2, R** — fourteenth century.

**Aug. b** — an Augsburg manuscript, fourteenth century.

**Dresd. a** — cod. 183 (Dresden), fourteenth century.

**M4** — Milan, Ambrosian Library, cod. C. 24 sup., fifteenth century.

*Collective symbols*

**r** — one or more of the manuscripts other than L. Used where a more particular statement is unnecessary; it keeps the note short and marks the paramount importance of L.

**MSS.** — after a reading: the reading is in all the manuscripts known to the editor.

*Other terms in the notes*

**1st hand, first hand** — the original scribe of L.

**first corrector, corrector** — a later hand that revised L.

**Schol., schol.** — the scholia in L.

**conject.** — conjectured by the scholar named.

**vulg.** — the vulgate, the reading of the ordinary printed texts before modern criticism.
