# title: Glossary of names in the Antigone
# lang: eng
# license: CC BY-SA 4.0

## Ἀντιγόνη
**Ἀντιγόνη, ἡ** Antigone, daughter of Oedipus and Iocasta, sister of Ismene, Eteocles and Polyneices; betrothed to Haemon.

## Ἰσμήνη
**Ἰσμήνη, ἡ** Ismene, Antigone's younger sister.

## Ἐτεοκλῆς
**Ἐτεοκλῆς, -έους, ὁ** Eteocles, son of Oedipus, who fell defending Thebes against his brother Polyneices.

## Πολυνείκης
**Πολυνείκης, -ους, ὁ** Polyneices, son of Oedipus, who led the Argive army against Thebes and fell at his brother's hand.

## Εὐρυδίκη
**Εὐρυδίκη, ἡ** Eurydice, wife of Creon and mother of Haemon.

## Μενοικεύς
**Μενοικεύς, -έως, ὁ** Menoeceus, father of Creon and Iocasta.

## Τειρεσίας
**Τειρεσίας, -ου, ὁ** Teiresias, the blind Theban seer.

## Λάιος
**Λάιος, ὁ** Laius, king of Thebes, father of Oedipus.

## Λαβδακίδης
**Λαβδακίδης, -ου, ὁ** a descendant of Labdacus, grandfather of Laius; the royal house of Thebes.

## Ἀμφίων
**Ἀμφίων, -ονος, ὁ** Amphion, husband of Niobe, who built the walls of Thebes with his lyre.

## Δρύας
**Δρύας, -αντος, ὁ** Dryas, father of Lycurgus, king of the Edonians.

## Ἠδωνοί
**Ἠδωνοί, οἱ** the Edonians, a Thracian people ruled by Lycurgus, who persecuted Dionysus.

## Φινείδης
**Φινείδης, -ου, ὁ** a son of Phineus, the Thracian king; his two sons were blinded by their stepmother.

## Σαλμυδησσός
**Σαλμυδησσός, ὁ** Salmydessus, a town on the Thracian coast of the Black Sea, home of Phineus.

## Σίπυλος
**Σίπυλος, ὁ** Mount Sipylus in Lydia, where Niobe was turned to stone.

## Διρκαῖος
**Διρκαῖος, -α, -ον** of Dirce, the spring and stream west of Thebes.

## Ἰσμηνός
**Ἰσμηνός, ὁ** the Ismenus, the river east of Thebes.

## Νυσαῖος
**Νυσαῖος, -α, -ον** of Nysa, the mountain sacred to Dionysus; here of Euboea.

## οὔποτε
**οὔποτε** adv. never, at no time.
