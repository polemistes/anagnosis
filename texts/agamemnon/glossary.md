# title: Glossary of names and rare words in the Agamemnon
# lang: eng
# license: CC BY-SA 4.0

## Ἀτρεύς
**Ἀτρεύς, -έως, ὁ** Atreus, son of Pelops, king of Argos, father of Agamemnon and Menelaus; he served his brother Thyestes the flesh of Thyestes' children at a feast, the crime that haunts his house.

## Πάρις
**Πάρις, -ιδος, ὁ** Paris, son of Priam, who carried off Helen, the wife of Menelaus, and so brought the war on Troy.

## Αἴγισθος
**Αἴγισθος, ὁ** Aegisthus, son of Thyestes, Clytemnestra's lover, who plots with her the murder of Agamemnon.

## Τευκρίς
**Τευκρίς, -ίδος, ἡ** (adj.) Teucrian, Trojan (from Teucer, the first king of the Troad): Τευκρίδʼ ἐπʼ αἶαν, to the Trojan land.

## Μεσσάπιον
**Μεσσάπιον, τό** Messapion, a mountain in Boeotia near the Euripus, one of the stations of the beacon.

## Ἀσωπός
**Ἀσωπός, ὁ** the Asopus, a river of Boeotia, whose plain the beacon-fire crosses.

## Κιθαιρών
**Κιθαιρών, -ῶνος, ὁ** Cithaeron, the mountain range between Boeotia and Attica, one of the stations of the beacon.

## Σαρωνικός
**Σαρωνικός, -ή, -όν** Saronic: the Saronic Gulf, between Attica and the Argolid, over which the beacon-fire leaps.

## ἀσπιδοστρόφος
**ἀσπιδοστρόφος, -ον** shield-wielding, handling the shield (Ag. 825, the Argive host leaping from the horse): the form Sidgwick prints; other editions have ἀσπιδηστρόφος.

## Λήδη
**Λήδη, ἡ** Leda, wife of Tyndareus, mother of Helen and Clytemnestra.

## Κασσάνδρη
**Κασσάνδρη, ἡ** Cassandra (= Κασσάνδρα), daughter of Priam, the prophetess whom Apollo condemned never to be believed; Agamemnon brings her home as his captive.

## Ἀλκμήνη
**Ἀλκμήνη, ἡ** Alcmena, mother of Heracles, who was sold into slavery (Ag. 1040: even the son of Alcmena bore the slave's yoke).

## Ἀχερούσιος
**Ἀχερούσιος, -α, -ον** of the Acheron, the river of the underworld; Ἀχερούσιαι ἀκταί, the banks of Acheron.

## δυσφροίμιος
**δυσφροίμιος, -ον** of ill-boding prelude or beginning (Ag. 1216, of Cassandra's prophecy).

## Ἀγαμεμνόνιος
**Ἀγαμεμνόνιος, -α, -ον** of Agamemnon (Ag. 1499, Ἀγαμεμνονίαν … ἄλοχον, Agamemnon's wife).

## Πλεισθενίδης
**Πλεισθενίδης, -ου, ὁ** son or descendant of Pleisthenes: the house of Atreus (Ag. 1569).

## Πελοπίδης
**Πελοπίδης, -ου, ὁ** son or descendant of Pelops: Atreus and his house (Ag. 1600).

## Πλεισθένης
**Πλεισθένης, -ους, ὁ** Pleisthenes, son (in other accounts grandson) of Pelops, from whom the house of Atreus is called Pleisthenid (Ag. 1602).

## Αὐλίς
**Αὐλίς, -ίδος, ἡ** Aulis, a harbour in Boeotia opposite Chalcis, where the Greek fleet lay windbound and Iphigeneia was sacrificed.

## οὔποτε
**οὔποτε** never (= οὔ ποτε).
