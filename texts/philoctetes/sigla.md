Jebb's note on the symbols of his critical apparatus:

**L** — cod. Laur. 32. 9 (first half of eleventh century).

**r** — one or more of the later MSS. This symbol is used where a more particular statement is unnecessary.

**MSS.** — after a reading, means that it is in all the MSS. known to the editor.

The other manuscripts the notes name are listed in this volume's Introduction, in the chapter "Manuscripts, editions, etc." (§ 1): the Parisian A, B, K, T (T giving the recension of Triclinius); the Florentine Γ, Lc, L², R; the Venetian V, V², V³; the Roman Vat. and Vat. b; and the London Harl. (Harleian). K, Lc and Harl. are described there; the others in the earlier volumes. S is the first corrector (diorthotes) of L.
