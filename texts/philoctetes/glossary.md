# title: Glossary of names in the Philoctetes
# lang: eng
# license: CC BY-SA 4.0

## Φιλοκτήτης
**Φιλοκτήτης, -ου, ὁ** Philoctetes, son of Poeas, of Malis; he lit the pyre of Heracles on Oeta and received his bow, and was put ashore on Lemnos by the Greeks, sick of a wound from a serpent's bite.

## Ποίας
**Ποίας, -αντος, ὁ** Poeas, king of the Malians, father of Philoctetes; ὁ Ποίαντος (παῖς), Philoctetes.

## Χρύση
**Χρύση, ἡ** Chrysè, a goddess with an open-air sanctuary near Lemnos, whose hidden serpent, its guardian, bit Philoctetes (194, 1326 ff.; Jebb's Introduction §§ 21-22).

## Ἀτρεύς
**Ἀτρεύς, -έως, ὁ** Atreus, father of Agamemnon and Menelaus; τῶν Ἀτρέως διπλῶν στρατηγῶν (1023 f.), the two sons of Atreus, the generals.

## Ἕλενος
**Ἕλενος, ὁ** Helenus, son of Priam, a seer, captured by Odysseus; his prophecy that Troy could not be taken without Philoctetes and his bow drives the play (604 ff., 1337 ff.).

## Λυκομήδης
**Λυκομήδης, -ους, ὁ** Lycomedes, king of Scyros, grandfather of Neoptolemus, who was brought up in his house (243).

## Σίγειον
**Σίγειον, τό** Sigeum, the promontory of the Troad at the mouth of the Hellespont, where Neoptolemus landed (355).

## Πακτωλός
**Πακτωλός, ὁ** the Pactolus, the gold-bearing river of Lydia on which Sardis stands (393).

## Πύλιος
**Πύλιος, -α, -ον** of Pylos; ὁ Πύλιος (Νέστωρ) (422), Nestor of Pylos.

## Ἀντίλοχος
**Ἀντίλοχος, ὁ** Antilochus, son of Nestor, fallen at Troy (425).

## Τεῦκρος
**Τεῦκρος, ὁ** Teucer, son of Telamon, brother of Ajax, the best archer of the Greeks at Troy (1057).

## Πάρις
**Πάρις, -ιδος, ὁ** Paris, son of Priam, cause of the war; Heracles foretells that Philoctetes will slay him with the bow (1426).

## Πεπάρηθος
**Πεπάρηθος, ἡ** Peparethus, an island off Thessaly, rich in vines (548 f.), the home the pretended merchant claims.

## Τραχίνιος
**Τραχίνιος, -α, -ον** of Trachis, the town of Malis under Mount Oeta; Τραχινία δειράς (491), the ridge of Trachis.

## Μαλιάς
**Μαλιάς, -άδος** (fem. adj.) Malian, of Malis, the home of Philoctetes; Μαλιάδες νύμφαι (725), the nymphs of Malis.

## Ἀσκληπίδης
**Ἀσκληπίδης, -ου, ὁ** (= Ἀσκληπιάδης) a son of Asclepius; οἱ Ἀσκληπίδαι (1333), his sons Machaon and Podalirius, physicians of the Greek army.

## παμβῶτις
**παμβῶτις, -ιδος** (fem. adj.) all-nourishing; παμβῶτι Γᾶ (391), Earth the all-nourishing, the Mother of the gods.

## οὔποτε
**οὔποτε** adv. never, at no time.
