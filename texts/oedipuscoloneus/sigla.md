Jebb's note on the symbols of his critical apparatus:

**L** — cod. Laur. 32. 9 (first half of eleventh century).

**r** — one or more of the later MSS. This symbol is used where a more particular statement is unnecessary.

**MSS.** — after a reading, means that it is in all the MSS. known to the editor.

The other manuscripts the notes name are described in this volume's Introduction, in the chapter "Manuscripts. Editions and commentaries" (§ 3): those Jebb collated himself, the Parisian A, B and T (T giving the recension of Triclinius); the six whose readings he gives from Elmsley's edition, F (Paris), R and R² (the Riccardian Library, Florence), L² (the Laurentian Library), Vat. (the Vatican) and Farn. (Naples); and, in a few places from his own notes, the Venetian V² and V³. S is the corrector (diorthotes) of L.
