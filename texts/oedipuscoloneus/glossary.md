# title: Glossary of names in the Oedipus at Colonus
# lang: eng

## Ἀντιγόνη
**Ἀντιγόνη, ἡ** Antigone, daughter of Oedipus and Iocasta, who has led her blind father in his wanderings.

## Ἰσμήνη
**Ἰσμήνη, ἡ** Ismene, the other daughter of Oedipus, who has kept him informed from Thebes.

## Πολυνείκης
**Πολυνείκης, -ους, ὁ** Polyneices, in this play the elder son of Oedipus, driven from Thebes by his brother; he comes from Argos with the Seven against Thebes and asks for his father's blessing.

## Ἐτεοκλῆς
**Ἐτεοκλῆς, -έους, ὁ** Eteocles, in this play the younger son of Oedipus (1295), who has driven out Polyneices and holds Thebes.

## Λάιος
**Λάιος, ὁ** Laius, king of Thebes, father of Oedipus, killed by him unknowing.

## Λαβδακίδης
**Λαβδακίδης, -ου, ὁ** a descendant of Labdacus, father of Laius; τὸ Λαβδακιδᾶν γένος (221), the house of Labdacus.

## Αἰγεύς
**Αἰγεύς, -έως, ὁ** Aegeus, king of Athens, father of Theseus; παῖ Αἰγέως, τέκνον Αἰγέως, Theseus.

## Πειρίθοος
**Πειρίθοος, ὁ** (Attic Περίθους) Peirithous, king of the Lapiths, sworn friend of Theseus, with whom he went down to Hades; Θησέως Περίθου τε … ξυνθήματα (1593 f.), the pledges of their compact.

## Θορίκιος
**Θορίκιος, -α, -ον** of Thoricus, an Attic deme; ὁ Θορίκιος πέτρος (1595), "the Thorician rock", one of the landmarks at the place of Oedipus' passing.

## Εὐμολπίδαι
**Εὐμολπίδαι, οἱ** the Eumolpidae, the Eleusinian priestly family descended from Eumolpus, who held the office of hierophant at the Mysteries (1053).

## Ῥῖπαι
**Ῥῖπαι, αἱ** the Rhipaean mountains, in the far north, whence the north wind blows (1248).

## Θεσσαλίς
**Θεσσαλίς, -ίδος** (fem. adj.) Thessalian; κυνῆ Θεσσαλίς (314), a Thessalian hat, broad-brimmed against the sun.

## Αἰτωλός
**Αἰτωλός, -ή, -όν** Aetolian; Αἰτωλὸς Οἰνέως τόκος Τυδεύς (1315 f.), Tydeus the Aetolian, son of Oeneus.

## Οἰνεύς
**Οἰνεύς, -έως, ὁ** Oeneus, king of Calydon in Aetolia, father of Tydeus.

## Ἐτέοκλος
**Ἐτέοκλος, ὁ** Eteoclus, an Argive, one of the Seven against Thebes (1316).

## Καπανεύς
**Καπανεύς, -έως, ὁ** Capaneus, one of the Seven, who boasts that he will burn Thebes to the ground (1318 f.).

## Ἀρκάς
**Ἀρκάς, -άδος, ὁ** an Arcadian; Παρθενοπαῖος Ἀρκάς (1320), Parthenopaeus, one of the Seven.

## Ἀταλάντη
**Ἀταλάντη, ἡ** Atalanta, the huntress, long unwed, mother of Parthenopaeus (1321 f.).

## αὐτόπετρος
**αὐτόπετρος, -ον** of natural rock; αὐτοπέτρου βήματος (192), the ledge of the native rock on which Oedipus is to sit.

## οὔποτε
**οὔποτε** adv. never, at no time.
