Starkie's critical apparatus, between two rules under the text (on some pages running on to the facing page), gives each item with the line number first; several items on one line are divided by ||. He quotes the two great manuscripts, which he collated in facsimile, Suidas and the Aldine, and the other manuscripts only where they are reported to give a better text. A reading in the text that is a conjecture is marked there with an asterisk, which this text leaves out; letters or words he supplies stand in pointed brackets ⟨ ⟩.

## The manuscripts

**R** — the Ravennas, Ravenna, the best manuscript of the *Clouds*; its besetting fault is the omission of words and lines.

**V** — the Venetus (Marcianus 474), Venice; in the *Clouds* a very corrupt text, perhaps not from R's archetype.

**A, C** — Parisian manuscripts; **Δ, Θ, Φ, Χ, Σ, Ψ, Ω** — Florentine (Laurentian); **M** — Milan (Ambrosian); **U** — Rome (Urbinas); **E** — Modena; **G** — Venice. Quoted only where they are reported to have a better text. **codd.** — the manuscripts; **al.**, **dett.** (*deteriores*) — other, later manuscripts.

**Su.** — Suidas, who quotes 355 verses of the play. **Ald.** — the Aldine edition (Venice 1498), which alone or with the later manuscripts often gives the right text. **schol.** — the scholia (schol. R, schol. V; *int.*, *ext.*: inside or outside the margin).

## Abbreviations of the apparatus

om. — omit, leave out · cp. — compare · sq., sqq. — and the following line(s) · crit. n. — critical note · comm. — the commentary.
