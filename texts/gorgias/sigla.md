[Anagnosis: These are the manuscripts cited in Burnet's critical apparatus, as he lists them at the head of the volume, with the names Bekker and Stallbaum gave them. The apparatus also cites ancient authors who quote the Gorgias or comment on it (Olympiodorus, Stobaeus, Aristides, Suidas and others) and the conjectures of modern scholars; *vulg.* is the text of the early printed editions, *al.* other manuscripts.]
