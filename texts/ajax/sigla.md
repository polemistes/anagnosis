Jebb's note on the symbols of his critical notes:

**L** — cod. Laur. 32. 9 (first half of eleventh century).

**r** — one or more of the later MSS. This symbol is used where a more particular statement is unnecessary.

**MSS.** — after a reading, means that it is in all the MSS. known to the editor.

The other manuscripts the notes name (A, Γ, T, L2, Pal., Mosq., Aug. b, Lb, Harl., Lips. and others) are those that Jebb describes in his Introduction, in the chapter "Manuscripts, editions, etc."
