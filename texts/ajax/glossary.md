# title: Glossary of names in the Ajax
# lang: eng
# license: CC BY-SA 4.0

## Τεῦκρος
**Τεῦκρος, ὁ** Teucer, son of Telamon and the Trojan captive Hesione, half-brother of Ajax, and the best archer among the Greeks at Troy.

## Τέκμησσα
**Τέκμησσα, ἡ** Tecmessa, daughter of the Phrygian Teleutas, the captive whom Ajax made his consort; mother of Eurysaces.

## Τελεύτας
**Τελεύτας, -αντος, ὁ** Teleutas, a Phrygian prince, father of Tecmessa.

## Σισυφίδης
**Σισυφίδης, -ου, ὁ** son of Sisyphus: a taunt for Odysseus, whom his enemies called the son of Sisyphus rather than of Laertes; τᾶς Σισυφιδᾶν γενεᾶς (190), of the brood of Sisyphus.

## Κυλλήνιος
**Κυλλήνιος, -α, -ον** (Doric Κυλλάνιος) of Cyllene, the mountain in Arcadia, haunt of Pan and birthplace of Hermes.

## Κνώσιος
**Κνώσιος, -α, -ον** of Cnossus in Crete, Cnossian.

## Θεστόρειος
**Θεστόρειος, -α, -ον** of Thestor: ὁ Θεστόρειος μάντις (801), Calchas, the son of Thestor.

## Ἀτρεύς
**Ἀτρεύς, -έως, ὁ** Atreus, son of Pelops, father of Agamemnon and Menelaus.

## Ἀλκμήνη
**Ἀλκμήνη, ἡ** Alcmena, wife of Amphitryon and mother of Heracles by Zeus; Ἀλκμήνης γόνος (1303), Heracles.

## οὔποτε
**οὔποτε** never (οὐ and ποτέ).
