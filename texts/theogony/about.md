The Greek text of Alois Rzach's Teubner edition of Hesiod's Theogony, with his critical apparatus, F. A. Paley's English commentary, preface and epitome of the poem, Hugh G. Evelyn-White's translation, and the Liddell–Scott dictionaries. Tap any word for its meaning and form. The syntax of the sentences is annotated by hand; the syntax trees were made on the Perseus Digital Library's text of the poem (H. G. Evelyn-White's Loeb edition, 1914), which differs in places from Rzach's text printed here, and where a sentence differs the syntax view shows both and marks the words. About two words in a hundred are not analysed as the treebank has them: where the printed text differs from the treebank's, the dictionary form and grammatical form were set by an automatic analyser, by rule or by an AI model.

The Greek text, the critical apparatus and Paley's commentary were read from scans of the printed editions by OCR and checked against the page images by AI models, in two readings. The translation is the Perseus Digital Library's digital text.
