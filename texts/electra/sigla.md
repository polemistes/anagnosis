L = cod. Laur. 32. 9 (first half of eleventh century). r = one or more of the later MSS. This symbol is used where a more particular statement is unnecessary. ‘MSS.,’ after a reading, means that it is in all the MSS. known to the editor.

[Anagnosis: the lines above are Jebb's note at the head of his critical apparatus. The other manuscripts that the apparatus names (A, Γ, T, L2, Pal. and others) he discusses in his Introduction, in the chapter "Manuscripts, editions, etc."]
