# title: Glossary of names in the Electra
# lang: eng
# license: CC BY-SA 4.0

## Αἴγισθος
**Αἴγισθος, ὁ** Aegisthus, son of Thyestes, lover of Clytaemnestra and with her the murderer of Agamemnon, whose throne in Mycenae he holds.

## Ἠλέκτρα
**Ἠλέκτρα, ἡ** Electra, daughter of Agamemnon and Clytaemnestra, sister of Orestes, Chrysothemis and Iphianassa.

## Χρυσόθεμις
**Χρυσόθεμις, -ιδος, ἡ** Chrysothemis, daughter of Agamemnon and Clytaemnestra, Electra's sister.

## Ἰφιάνασσα
**Ἰφιάνασσα, ἡ** Iphianassa, a daughter of Agamemnon, named with Chrysothemis in *Il.* 9. 145.

## Πυλάδης
**Πυλάδης, -ου, ὁ** Pylades, son of Strophius of Phocis, the friend and companion of Orestes.

## Πελοπίδης
**Πελοπίδης, -ου, ὁ** a descendant of Pelops; οἱ Πελοπίδαι, the house of Pelops.

## Μυρτίλος
**Μυρτίλος, ὁ** Myrtilus, charioteer of Oenomaus, whom Pelops threw into the sea: the first crime of the house.

## Φανοτεύς
**Φανοτεύς, -έως, ὁ** Phanoteus, a Phocian, friend of Aegisthus and Clytaemnestra, from whom the old servant pretends to come (45).

## Νιόβη
**Νιόβη, ἡ** (Doric Νιόβα) Niobe, daughter of Tantalus, who boasted over Leto; her children slain, she was turned to stone on Mount Sipylus, ever weeping.

## Αἰνιάν
**Αἰνιάν, -ᾶνος, ὁ** one of the Aenianes, a people of southern Thessaly; an Aenianian.

## Βαρκαῖος
**Βαρκαῖος, -α, -ον** of Barca, a Greek city in Libya near Cyrene; Barcaean.

## Κρισαῖος
**Κρισαῖος, -α, -ον** of Crisa near Delphi, in whose plain the Pythian chariot races were run; Crisaean.

## πάγχρυσος
**πάγχρυσος, -ον** all of gold, all-golden.
