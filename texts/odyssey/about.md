The Greek text of T. W. Allen's Oxford edition with its critical apparatus, A. T. Murray's translation, the commentary of Merry, Riddell and Monro, Autenrieth's Homeric Dictionary and the Liddell–Scott dictionaries. Tap any word for its meaning and form. The syntax of the sentences is annotated by hand; the syntax trees were made on the Perseus Digital Library's text of the poem (A. T. Murray's Loeb edition, 1919), which differs in places from Allen's text printed here, and where a sentence differs the syntax view shows both and marks the words. About two words in a hundred are not analysed as the treebank has them: where the printed text differs from the treebank's, the dictionary form and grammatical form were set by an automatic analyser, by rule or by an AI model.

The Greek text and the critical apparatus were typed from scans of Allen's edition by AI models, page by page, in one reading. The translation and the commentary are the Perseus Digital Library's digital texts of the printed books; they have not been compared with the books.
