Jebb's note on the symbols of his critical apparatus:

**L** — cod. Laur. 32. 9 (first half of eleventh century).

**r** — one or more of the later MSS. This symbol is used where a more particular statement is unnecessary.

**MSS.** — after a reading, means that it is in all the MSS. known to the editor.

The other manuscripts the notes name are the twelve that Jebb lists in his Introduction, in the chapter "Manuscripts, editions, etc.": the Parisian A, B, K, T; the Florentine Lc, L², R; the Venetian V, V², V³; the Roman Vat.; and the London Harl. (collated by Porson). He describes them in his volumes on the Oedipus Tyrannus, the Oedipus at Colonus and the Philoctetes. S is the first corrector of L.
