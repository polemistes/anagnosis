# title: Glossary of names in the Trachiniae
# lang: eng

## Ἰόλη
**Ἰόλη, ἡ** Iole, daughter of Eurytus, king of Oechalia; taken captive by Heracles and sent home to Trachis with Lichas.

## Λίχας
**Λίχας, -α, ὁ** Lichas, the herald of Heracles, who brings the captives to Trachis and carries the poisoned robe back to his master.

## Νέσσος
**Νέσσος, ὁ** Nessus, the Centaur who carried travellers over the Evenus; shot by Heracles, he gave Deianeira the blood that she took for a love-charm.

## Οἰνεύς
**Οἰνεύς, -έως, ὁ** Oeneus, king of Calydon in Aetolia, father of Deianeira; παῖ (τέκνον) Οἰνέως, Deianeira.

## Ἀλκμήνη
**Ἀλκμήνη, ἡ** (Doric Ἀλκμήνα) Alcmena, wife of Amphitryon and mother of Heracles by Zeus; ὁ Ἀλκμήνης παῖς (τόκος), Heracles.

## Ἴφιτος
**Ἴφιτος, ὁ** Iphitus, son of Eurytus, whom Heracles threw from the heights of Tiryns; for this deed Zeus sold him as a slave to Omphale.

## Εὐρυσθεύς
**Εὐρυσθεύς, -έως, ὁ** Eurystheus, king of Argos (Mycenae and Tiryns), who laid the labours on Heracles.

## Ὀμφάλη
**Ὀμφάλη, ἡ** Omphale, the Lydian queen whom Heracles served for a year as a bought slave.

## Εὐρύτειος
**Εὐρύτειος, -α, -ον** of Eurytus: ἡ Εὐρυτεία (260), his city Oechalia; ἡ Εὐρυτεία παρθένος (1219), Iole.

## Εὐβοΐς
**Εὐβοΐς, -ΐδος, ἡ** (also spelt Εὐβοιίς) Euboean (fem.): Εὐβοῒς χώρα (74), the land of Euboea; ἀκτή τις Εὐβοιίς (237), a headland of Euboea.

## Κηναῖος
**Κηναῖος, -α, -ον** of Cenaeum, the north-western headland of Euboea, where Heracles founded altars to Zeus; Κηναῖος Ζεύς (238), Zeus of Cenaeum; τὸ Κήναιον (753), the headland itself.

## Πλευρών
**Πλευρών, -ῶνος, ἡ** Pleuron, a city of Aetolia, where Deianeira lived in her father's house before her marriage.

## Αἰτωλίς
**Αἰτωλίς, -ίδος, ἡ** Aetolian (fem.): Αἰτωλὶς γυνή (8), an Aetolian woman.

## Οἰνιάδαι
**Οἰνιάδαι, -ῶν, οἱ** Oeniadae, a town of Acarnania near the mouth of the Achelous: Ἀχελῷος ἀπʼ Οἰνιαδᾶν (509).

## Εὔηνος
**Εὔηνος, ὁ** the Evenus, a river of Aetolia, at which Nessus carried travellers across for hire.

## Κρήσιος
**Κρήσιος, -α, -ον** Cretan: πέλαγος Κρήσιον (116–120), the Cretan sea.

## Τίρυνς
**Τίρυνς, -υνθος, ἡ** Tiryns, the city in the Argolid, the old home of Heracles, where Alcmena now lives (ἐπακτία Τίρυνς, 1151–1152).

## Τιρύνθιος
**Τιρύνθιος, -α, -ον** of Tiryns: Τιρυνθία κλιτύς (270–271), the hill of Tiryns, from whose heights Heracles threw Iphitus.

## Ἐρυμάνθιος
**Ἐρυμάνθιος, -α, -ον** of Erymanthus, the mountain in Arcadia: Ἐρυμάνθιος θήρ (1097), the Erymanthian boar, one of the labours of Heracles.

## Λερναῖος
**Λερναῖος, -α, -ον** of Lerna, the marsh near Argos: Λερναία ὕδρα (574, 1094), the hydra of Lerna, whose poison was on the arrows of Heracles.

## οὔποτε
**οὔποτε** never (οὐ and ποτέ).
