## Παῦλος
**Παῦλος, ου, ὁ** Paul, Paulus.

## Ἰωάννης
**Ἰωάννης, ου, ὁ** John: the Baptist, the apostle, a member of the Sanhedrin, or John Mark.

## ἐνώπιον
**ἐνώπιον** before the face of, in the presence of, in the eyes of.

## Μωϋσῆς
**Μωσῆς, έως, ὁ** Moses; met: the books of Moses, the Pentateuch.

## Ἰερουσαλήμ
**Ἱερουσαλήμ, ἡ** (Aramaic form), Jerusalem, the capital of Palestine: hence Judaism, and allegorically, Christendom, the Christian Church.

## Ἀβραάμ
**Ἀβραάμ, ὁ** Abraham, progenitor of the Hebrew race.

## Ἰσραήλ
**Ἰσραήλ, ὁ** (Hebrew), Israel, surname of Jacob, then the Jewish people, the people of God.

## πρεσβύτερος
**πρεσβύτερος, α, ον** elder, usually used as subst.; an elder, a member of the Sanhedrin, an elder of a Christian assembly.

## Ἱεροσόλυμα
**Ἱεροσόλυμα, ατος, τά, ἡ** the Greek form of the Hebrew name: Jerusalem.

## Γαλιλαία
**Γαλιλαία, ας, ἡ** Galilee, a district towards the southern end of the Roman province Syria; the northern division of Palestine.

## Δαυίδ
**Δαυίδ, ὁ** David, King of Israel.

## Πιλᾶτος
**Πιλᾶτος, ου, ὁ** Pilate.

## οὐχί
**οὐχί** by no means, not at all.

## Ἰουδαία
**Ἰουδαία, ας, ἡ** Judea, a Roman province, capital Jerusalem.

## Ἰούδας
**Ἰούδας, α, ὁ** Judah, Judas, Jude.

## Ἡρῴδης
**Ἡρῴδης, ου, ὁ** Herod; four persons are called by this name: Herod the Great, Herod Antipas, Herod Agrippa, and Herod Agrippa the younger.

**Ἰάκωβος, ου, ὁ** James, (a) the Small, son of Alphaeus, and one of the Twelve, (b) half-brother of Jesus, (c) father (?) of Jude, (d) son of Zebedee, and brother of John, one of the Twelve, killed A.D. 44.

**παραγίνομαι** (a) I come on the scene, appear, come, (b) with words expressing destination: I present myself at, arrive at, reach.
