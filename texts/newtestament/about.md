The Greek New Testament in the SBL edition of Michael W. Holmes with its apparatus and the Berean Standard Bible. Tap any word for its meaning and form in Abbott-Smith's and Dodson's lexicons of the New Testament and the Liddell–Scott dictionaries, or hold it for the syntax of its sentence; the lemmas and morphology are those of the MACULA Greek project. Its syntax groups the words into phrases; the links from word to word and the names of the relations shown here are computed from it by this app.

The Greek text, its apparatus, the word analyses and the translation are the publishers' own digital editions.
