## Πενθεύς
**Πενθεύς, -έως, ὁ** Pentheus, king of Thebes, torn to pieces by the Bacchants whom Dionysus led (Eum. 26).

## Πλειστός
**Πλειστός, -οῦ, ὁ** the Pleistus, the river of the valley below Delphi (Eum. 27).

## Φινεύς
**Φινεύς, -έως, ὁ** Phineus, the blind Thracian king whose food the Harpies snatched away (Eum. 50).

## Φλεγραῖος
**Φλεγραῖος, -α, -ον** of Phlegra, the plain where the gods fought the Giants (Eum. 295).

## Φέρης
**Φέρης, -ητος, ὁ** Pheres, father of Admetus, in whose house Apollo cheated the Fates to save Admetus' life (Eum. 723).

## σφε
**σφε** (enclitic pronoun) him, her, them; here *him*, Orestes (Eum. 234).

## φύγδα
**φύγδα** (adv.) in flight, by fleeing (Eum. 256).

## οὐπώποτε
**οὐπώποτε** (adv.) never yet, never at any time (Eum. 616).

## οἰοῖ
**οἰοῖ** (interj.) a cry of grief, ah! alas! (Eum. 841, 874).
