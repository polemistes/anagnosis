# Privacy policy for Anagnosis

Anagnosis is an ancient Greek text reader for Android, published by Robert Emil Berge.

**Anagnosis does not collect any data.** The app works entirely offline. It does not
connect to the internet, it contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting
libraries, and it does not ask for any permissions.

The only information the app stores is kept on your own device: your reading position
in each text, your display preferences (font, text size, colours, line numbers) and the
notes you write on passages of the texts. The app itself never sends this information
anywhere, and it is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

If backup is turned on in your device's settings, Android includes your notes and
preferences in the device's backup and when you move to a new device; this is done by
Android under Google's terms, not by the app. The texts and dictionaries are not part of
the backup. You can also export your notes on a text to a file yourself, which is saved
where you choose.

Purchases are handled by Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy policy. The
app itself does not receive any personal information about the buyer.

If you have questions, write to develop@robertemilberge.no.

Last updated: 13 October 2026.
