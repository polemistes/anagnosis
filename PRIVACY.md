# Privacy policy for Anagnosis

Anagnosis is an ancient Greek text reader for Android, published by Robert Emil Berge.

**Anagnosis does not collect any data.** The app works entirely offline. It does not
connect to the internet, and Android does not allow it to: the app does not have the
internet permission. It contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting libraries.
Google's billing library, which the app needs in order to sell texts, includes code that
sends diagnostic reports to Google; since the app has no internet permission, these
reports cannot leave your device.

The only information the app stores is kept on your own device: your reading position
in each text, your display preferences (font, text size, colours, line numbers), the
notes you write on passages of the texts, and the list of texts you have bought. The app
itself never sends this information anywhere, and it is deleted when the app is
uninstalled.

If backup is turned on in your device's settings, Android includes your notes and
preferences in the device's backup and when you move to a new device; this is done by
Android under Google's terms, not by the app. The texts and dictionaries are not part of
the backup. You can also export your notes on a text to a file yourself, which is saved
where you choose.

**Buying texts.** Texts are bought inside the app through Google Play. The payment, and
the download of a bought text, are handled by the Google Play app on your device, under
Google's own terms and privacy policy. Anagnosis only asks Google Play which texts your
Google account has bought, so that it can show them and fetch them again on a new
device; it receives no personal information about you. For this the app declares a few
permissions that Android grants without asking: the billing permission, which lets it
ask Google Play about purchases, and those Google Play's download service needs to bring a
bought text onto the device in the background.

If you have questions, write to develop@robertemilberge.no.
